Do not thread this keyword into `/api/estate/*` or unrelated Site routers in R373. Assign **R374** to the broader development-access composition gap in estate roots and other Site reads, for a separate security audit and slice before real principal rollout; inventory every affected `create_app` route, define one principal policy, and prove denied reads and lists there. R374 is a follow-up register ID, not a claim that R373 secures those routes.

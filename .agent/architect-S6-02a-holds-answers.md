## F4 - continuation after INCONCLUSIVE

A plan with no outcome may be superseded while evaluation is pending, subject to the existing freeze preconditions and replacement cutoff. A plan whose latest **settled** outcome is INCONCLUSIVE may also be superseded for the **same APPLIED Action**. Freeze a new immutable plan ID with `supersedes_plan_id`; never edit or erase the old plan, outcome, selection or event history. After the observable later-run cutoff in `D-2026-10-16-s602a-cutoff`, the new plan must use a new later window beginning strictly after the prior later window ends and satisfying all normal alignment, covering-Foundation and comparable-baseline checks. It may reuse the baseline only if that baseline still passes currentness and all freeze preconditions. Before the cutoff, the existing bounds-reuse rule applies. An identical original-freeze retry still returns its original ID; a new freeze key cannot silently replace it.

Replanning is a continuation of the original Action, not a new Action acceptance or a way around the battery-scoped guard. The old INCONCLUSIVE outcome remains historical; the new plan starts PENDING and the Action remains unresolved. Freeze and evaluation each serialize under the existing trial lock. Only the evaluator's later final current outcome can append RESOLVED and release the ordinary hold under the existing VERIFIED or target-miss-only INEFFECTIVE rule. A settled guardrail failure, a currently VERIFIED or target-miss-only INEFFECTIVE plan, a withdrawal awaiting reassessment, and a permanent prior-trial overlap are not eligible for this INCONCLUSIVE continuation. Complete a pending reassessment first; do not infer INCONCLUSIVE from an unfinished read state. There is no manual or SYSTEM withdrawal command for an INCONCLUSIVE trial in this version.

Implement this in S6-02a before merge. Otherwise its shipped INCONCLUSIVE public replay cannot lead to the later reserve-aware S6-02b trial, and a real inconclusive Action is stranded indefinitely. Prove pending and settled-INCONCLUSIVE replacement, the post-cutoff new-window rule, retained history/idempotency, and unchanged battery hold and negative cases on Postgres.

## F2 - evidence attributable to a withdrawn closure

The permanent `PRIOR_TRIAL_REASSESSMENT_OVERLAP` block is justified only by an identified set of accepted public readings that **caused the particular RESOLVED closure to lose its pinned verification basis**. At withdrawal, persist the exact accepted reading/envelope IDs, acceptance times, affected window and the old/new selected S3 assessment or direct verification-input IDs used to establish that causal change. Eligible readings must have been accepted after that closure's RESOLVED event and before its RESOLUTION_WITHDRAWN event; intersect a pinned half-open baseline or later window; and match that plan's frozen tenant, Site, role and bound asset for the affected role. The asset test includes every frozen bound load asset and excludes unbound assets. A same-Site report from another role or asset may trip the replacement cutoff, but it is not thereby invalidating evidence.

Attribute only readings whose arrival materially changed a pinned S3 selection or the accepted public input set on which that closure's target, comparability, coverage, guardrail or source resolution depended. Compare the closed basis with the reassessed basis while holding unrelated Foundation, source-classification and rule changes fixed; a mere time/window match or a newly listed but unused row is insufficient. Several jointly causal readings may be recorded together. A withdrawal caused solely by a Foundation change, source reclassification, S3/S4 worker state, or other non-reading basis change has an empty invalidating-reading set. If causality cannot be established from persisted IDs and basis, leave the ordinary unresolved hold in force and report reassessment, but do not create a permanent overlap block by guessing.

For an already accepted later Action at the same battery scope, record permanent overlap only when its acceptance was **after** acceptance of at least one such invalidating reading and **before** withdrawal of the closure it invalidated. An Action accepted before that evidence arrived does not overlap. Keep exact links to the closure, withdrawn outcome, later Action and causal accepted IDs. Re-evaluation must not broaden the set to every late reading in either window or turn a Foundation-only withdrawal into an overlap. This does not waive the separate `PRIOR_TRIAL_GUARDRAIL_FAILED` block.

Implement the attribution and overlap predicate in S6-02a before merge because this is a permanent block with no override. Prove bound-role/asset causal evidence on both sides of the acceptance timing boundary, unbound and unrelated-role reports, Foundation/source-only withdrawals, and an indeterminate attribution that fails closed as an ordinary unresolved hold without manufacturing a permanent overlap.

## Q3 — demonstration provenance

The current label is acceptable for the reviewed, isolated S4-02 demonstration and its fail-closed `Source not confirmed` behavior, but it can falsely label a real KOBO-001 Finding on 2026-10-23 and is not tenant scoped. Add an immutable, tenant/Site-scoped, server-authorized batch/publication source designation with fixture/spec version and publication identity; bind it to accepted evidence IDs and carry the cited evidence's designation into Finding/consequence reads. Gateway credentials or client-declared text alone must not confer authorship. Mixed or unresolvable inputs must show a cautious source state, not claim that the whole Finding is demonstration data or real measurements. Retire the date heuristic once this path is live.

**Code:** yes, a source-provenance follow-on spanning S2 ingestion and S4 reads/UI. It must land **before any real-Site pilot and before W1 exit**; until then the current demonstration label is evidence only for the bounded S4-02 fixture, not a general source-authorship capability. **Owner input:** ask the owner to approve the visible wording for mixed/unknown provenance and the pilot data designation at the W1 checkpoint.

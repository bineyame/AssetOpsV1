# Planner questions for Architect — S4-02 (2026-10-13)

## Q1 — Is the proposed `fuel-cost-1` upper multiplier acceptable as a conditional model bound?

The current Foundation `specific-fuel-consumption` is a point L/kWh value with no uncertainty field; legacy `analytics/dispatch.py` has `SFC_UNCERTAINTY = 1/10`, but `dispatch-3.1` intentionally withholds fuel. S4-02b proposes a **new versioned `fuel-cost-1` model parameter** `sfc_upper_multiplier = 1.10` and computes `quantity_high = Σ(max accepted mean generator kW covering each candidate segment × supported segment upper hours × time-valid Foundation L/kWh × 1.10)`, with zero lower bound. The UI would state “under the fuel-cost-1 model bound,” not a physical maximum or savings claim. No segment with incomplete accepted power/duration/SFC receives an amount. Is 1.10 an acceptable reviewed conditional parameter for this first bounded fixture, and what provenance should its version record?

Options: **A (recommended)** ratify 1.10 as a deliberately conditional, reviewed `fuel-cost-1` parameter with its model/fixture provenance and the above wording; **B** require a separate calibrated uncertainty source/contract before any positive upper amount; **C** use an expressly conditional point-rate amount without hard-bound wording, which would require a distinct label and acceptance rule. Implementation can proceed fail closed on B: S4-02a and S4-02b store/read/UI/unknown paths can be built, but no positive bounded amount or W1 `financials:1#a` closeout until the upper model parameter is approved. The Planner recommends A because it is explicit, testable and separate from the legacy computation; the independent S4-02b reviewer must still check the formula and visible limits.

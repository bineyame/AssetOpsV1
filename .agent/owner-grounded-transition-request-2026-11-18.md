## Coordinator's view, given to the owner (coordinator's analysis, not the owner's; weigh on merit)

The coordinator agreed it makes sense and is close to what the roadmap already intends (reuse matrix: 40 kept, 22 reworked, outcome map → structure registry, work cases and the trial action kept as carriers), but it changes the emphasis and the order: make today's signal → finding → action → verification path the delivery vehicle from the start, re-grounded on the factor structure, instead of building Layer 4 beside it and switching later. The coordinator named four risks to design against:
1. Emulator data is not economic benefit. Emulated sources (source-shaped billing, plan, quote exports) give demonstrable and testable flows; benefit is realised only on an operator's real records. Labels must say which (D6).
2. Direction of causality. The owner's earlier correction (feedback file, fourth message): outcomes are observed bottom-up through structurally defined factors, never detected at the top and explained afterwards. Findings must attach to factors (an observed deviation or condition on a factor key), not become the spine again.
3. One structure, not two. If findings, outcomes map and the factor structure each keep their own model, they diverge. One registry, with findings and actions keyed to it.
4. The honest-figure rules already in force (gross exposure is not a valued option; capacity without a customer is not an opportunity) must survive the re-grounding.

## Assumptions, authority and time

- `FinancialAssumptionRevision` has an immutable ID, tenant ID, optional Site ID, stable `assumption_key`, monotonically increasing revision number per scope/key, ISO 4217 currency where money is involved, canonical unit, Decimal value, basis kind and source reference, half-open `[valid_from, valid_to)`, server `recorded_at` and supersession time/reference. The first S4-02 write/read is **Site scoped**; tenant-scope rows and precedence over Site rows require a later explicit rule. One currently known revision per Site/key may cover a given valid instant. A correction appends a revision and closes recorded knowledge of the prior one; it never changes the old value or a pinned consequence.
- The first priced key is `diesel_price_per_litre` in currency/L, with basis explicitly `AUTHORED_ESTIMATE`, `CONTRACT_RATE` or `RECORDED_PURCHASE` and evidence appropriate to that label. A typed `maintenance_rate_per_generator_hour` or `maintenance_cost_per_start` may be stored only with its own source/model and matching quantity; it cannot be folded into fuel or called actual maintenance expenditure from candidate minutes alone. A discretionary alternative price is `SCENARIO`, not the operational price. No default price, silent tenant fallback, implicit FX, negative rate or conversion from unlike units is allowed.
- The write route derives `actor_kind`, `actor_id`, `author_id` (human only), channel, authorization basis and `recorded_at` on the server under `A-actor-identity`. A client-supplied identity is ignored/refused, not trusted. If there is no authenticated human path, disable the human price edit; a labelled, server-authorized `SYSTEM` demo publication may use stable service-principal/job/fixture provenance but must not impersonate a person. Use an expected prior revision/idempotency key and reject conflicting validity or concurrent edits. A future attestation requires proof bound to exact content/version.
- Select a price valid for the **whole** assessed consequence window and known at the chosen recorded cutoff. A future-dated price does not reprice yesterday. The demo edit that reprices an old Finding must be an explicit, audited correction whose validity covers that old window; it creates a new assumption and consequence revision. If a window crosses a price boundary, S4-02 either uses a reviewed partition with separately cited quantities per segment or remains unpriced; this first slice must not silently use the current price for the full interval. Pinned IDs reproduce the old result.

## Quantity, range and consequence

- The source technical claim is one exact, currently supported mini-grid `FindingRevision` for candidate generator-support segments, with its selected primary and all supporting `AssessmentRevision` IDs, accepted public evidence, model/cohort, half-open period and Foundation/binding basis. `CANDIDATE_AVOIDABLE` minutes are an upper bound on potentially avoidable runtime, not liters saved. S3-01's current `dispatch-3.1` read explicitly withholds a fuel/money claim; S4-02 must review and version a separate `fuel-cost-1` derivation from accepted generator-power coverage during candidate segments and the selected Foundation's specific fuel consumption in L/kWh. Do not import legacy `dispatch-2` fuel outputs, use private Lab truth, infer constant load, interpolate missing intervals or price Indeterminate/Necessary minutes. If those inputs or a defensible model bound are absent, state quantity unavailable and show no amount.
- For the first supported case, retain **unrounded** nonnegative quantity bounds in liters and their evidence/configuration/formula digest. Zero is the lower bound on *potentially avoidable* fuel, because candidate does not prove the counterfactual; the upper bound is the reviewed model's fuel estimate for covered candidate operation under the stated configured rate/uncertainty. Label the result “modelled, assumption-conditioned potential fuel-cost exposure”, never fuel actually saved, recovered value or a forecast that operation will change. If the rate has no defensible upper bound, do not pretend its point value is a physical upper bound: either publish an expressly conditional point-rate scenario with no hard-bound wording or leave the amount unavailable until the model supplies a reviewed bound. The Planner must fixture both the bounded and unavailable cases.
- `FinancialConsequenceRevision` is append-only under tenant/Site, Finding ID + exact FindingRevision ID, consequence type, selected assessment revision IDs, technical quantity bounds/unit/source digest, every assumption revision ID used, formula version, basis kind, overlap group, period, limitations, amount low/high and currency, plus a separate canonical basis digest. The technical design's single `quantity` field may retain a labelled reference amount, but the range needs explicit `quantity_low/high` (or an equivalent immutable typed bounds structure); no scalar is displayed as a proved saving. Recompute under a changed valid price appends a new consequence revision for the **same** FindingRevision and technical quantity/basis. It never mutates the Finding, assessment, evidence or old consequence. A changed FindingRevision needs a separately linked consequence. Identical basis digest is idempotent.
- Multiply compatible Decimal quantity bounds by the nonnegative price **before** display rounding. Store unrounded Decimal results; round displayed low downward and high upward to the currency's minor unit, and use unrounded compatible values for any permitted total. No mixed currency/unit, basis, window, overlap group, Finding revision or scenario/operational sum. This S4-02 slice should show the one consequence line and its exclusions, not a portfolio total. `UNPRICED` means a supported technical quantity with no valid price: amount and currency are null, not zero. `QUANTITY_UNAVAILABLE` means the technical derivation cannot be supported; `STALE/PENDING` means the consequence has not caught up with a selected Finding or assumption revision; an old revision is labelled historical/superseded. A pinned historical row remains inspectable with exact IDs and never masquerades as current.

## G-02 C-c: missing-kind rule

`missing_kind` identifies the **first absent authority needed to make the claim**, not merely the eventual source. Use `contract` when the record type, who may author it, validity/meaning or read/decision rule is not yet agreed; use `data_source` when that contract exists but its real producer, feed or records are absent; `decision` is a pending reviewed product/portion choice over existing inputs, `build` an agreed unimplemented path, and `visual` an agreed claim needing presentation. A source may remain `absent` under a `contract` gap. The commissioning gap says no commissioning record type exists, so change `G-readiness-commissioning.missing_kind` from `data_source` to `contract` **now**, without altering its visible “Not recorded” state, scope, status, allocation or W1 ranking. At W1-exit allocation, the owner still decides whether/when to establish the record and acquisition workflow; after a contract exists, a separately traceable missing producer may become a data-source gap. This clarifies only the commissioning example in `D-2026-10-12-mock-gap-closure-mechanism`.

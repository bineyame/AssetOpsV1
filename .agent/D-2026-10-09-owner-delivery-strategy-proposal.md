## Proposed decision text

Decision: `D-2026-10-09-owner-delivery-strategy`. Owner product decision on delivery order, fidelity target, prioritisation and autonomy. It supersedes no technical decision. Product choices never satisfy a technical prerequisite, owner review, independent review, lineage proof or cutover gate.

**Goal.** Deliver a demo faithful to `frontend/mockups/v69/index.html`, from persisted source-to-claim data. Mock values do not become product data; unsupported content remains honestly unavailable.

**Story-first waves.** W1 is the current S1–S7 mini-grid queue, with a mock comparison on each supported screen slice. W2 adds thin cold-chain and EV spines, one Site each, on the S2–S4 backbone: Foundation, evidence, one assessment, one Finding and bounded consequence, with cold-chain excursion/backup response and EV interrupted-session scenarios and their simulated worlds/fixtures. W3 builds Portfolio and global Findings (Cost/Risks/Opportunities), Actions, Evidence and Financials once, with all three domains present. W4 onward deepens remaining mini-grid objectives, domain work, Incident/Maintenance after their linked Findings, Asset root, onboarding and Settings. The Architect proposes exact manifest IDs for each wave at its start; the owner approves them before that wave's new implementation is selected. Shared FindingIndex, CapabilityReadiness, SourceHealth, money-comparability, route and actor-identity contracts may be decided earlier for all three domains, while implementation stays in the active wave.

**Fidelity.** Each wave targets `Real with honest gaps` for owner-approved core panels. `Real` remains the eventual target for each screen not explicitly dropped: every panel real or owner-approved withheld, with interaction, navigation and V-01 acceptance. Proposed dropped list: **none**. Proposed indefinitely deferred list: **none**. These are proposed values, not owner approval of every manifest screen or its tier. An unassigned screen retains a planned wave or an explicit later-wave allocation before ranking; no empty route or illustrative value counts as real. The existing S1 owner UI acceptance remains evidence for S1 only; the W1 start decision is prospective for remaining W1 work, never backdated.

Candidate W1 manifest IDs represented by the current queue and V-01 are `sites`, `foundation`, `readiness`, `sld`, `lab`, `evidence`, `evidence-analysis`, `dispatch`, `analysis`, `finding`, `finding-evidence`, `financials`, `assumptions`, `mini`, `changes`, `recommendation`, `verification`, `sitework`, `action-overview`, `health` and `asset` (all exist in the manifest). This is a proposed screen set, not a claim that every panel is supported; the Architect must attach the exact core-panel IDs and owner demo values at the W1 confirmation gate. Screens outside that set still need an explicit planned wave before ranking.

**Selection within the active wave.** Publish each candidate's raw `ready-now`, `closure-unlock`, reach, distance and effort facts beside the score. First enforce technical readiness and owner-approved wave scope. For eligible candidates use `score = 0.4 × demo + 0.3 × panels + 0.2 × reach − 0.1 × effort`, with owner demo value 1–5 per approved screen (a task touching several screens takes the highest approved value and lists the contributing screens), effort S=1/M=3/L=5, panels = min(5, number of distinct newly ready owner-approved core panels), and reach = min(5, number of distinct downstream subject areas/domains with an identified consumer). Count a panel once; publish uncapped counts as raw facts. Treat `closure-unlock` as the distinct panels made ready if this candidate and its named open technical upstream gates close; do not add it to the score until those gates really close. Recompute after each recorded decision, accepted task or closed fixture. Tie-break equal scores by lower unresolved technical-prerequisite distance, then lower effort, then stable manifest/task ID. Product-node `priority` stores the wave ID, not a score.

**Autonomy and stops.** After wave-start owner input, the coordinator may close out accepted work, recalculate, select an eligible top candidate, refine, build, obtain independent review, and merge under `.ai/WORKFLOW.md`. Only the coordinator records review outcomes and owner approvals after reading the underlying evidence; no Implementer commissions its own review. A `USER_REVIEW_REQUIRED` checkpoint blocks that task's acceptance/merge until owner review is recorded. Stop for owner at wave start (IDs, core panels, demo values), panel withholding, S2/S4/S6/S7 review checkpoints and each wave exit, screen dropping, contested ranking, or scoring-weight change. Also stop on checker failure, a second review rejection of the same task, or any real claim lacking lineage. Proposed contested ranking: the top two eligible scores differ by at most 10% of `max(abs(top score), 1)`; a candidate estimated **larger than L** (more than the L=5 bucket) must be split or sent to the owner. No silent override of a stopped gate.

**Current review routing.** Preserve the owner's specific S2 instruction: fresh Claude independent review per slice, one Codex review of the S2-01c/S2-02 whole, and the S2 Evidence owner checkpoint. Do not infer a new Codex review per slice from this autonomy loop. Decision requests go to the active Architect under `.ai/ROLE_CONFIG.md`; owner weighing is requested with a reason when product authority is needed.

**Product-node recording.** Once W1 is confirmed, record its approved nodes as `Real with honest gaps`, `priority: W1`, with exact approved core panels, owner evidence and demo values. Other nodes remain `pending owner` with a non-authoritative planned-wave field until their wave starts. Do not pre-mark all current W1 nodes real merely because S1 is merged. A node's status describes its approved target, not completion of every panel; panel evidence and `to_real` gates remain separate.

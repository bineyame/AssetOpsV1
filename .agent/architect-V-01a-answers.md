## 2. Configuration Readiness colour (Q3)

At the selected effective Foundation/binding version, green “Configured” or “Ready to report (configuration)” means every required role is configured and there are no Foundation configuration gaps; amber “Needs configuration” or “Incomplete” means a known required role/control/binding or Foundation declaration is missing. If the binding/configuration basis is absent or cannot be resolved, retain neutral “Unknown”; an optional unbound channel must not alone turn a complete required-role verdict amber. Apply these same typed meanings to role/table badges (configured green, not bound/not configured amber, Unknown neutral), with visible words and a nearby legend/scope line. Do not colour publication/version badges as health or make green imply contact, received records, accepted Evidence, claim readiness, commissioned equipment, or good asset condition.

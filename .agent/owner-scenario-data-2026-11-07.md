# Owner guidance on scenario data (2026-11-07)

Typed by the owner in the coordinator session, verbatim, after the D0 result and the missing-fuel decision:

"dont be limited by existing data, data that is reasonable and can reflect a situation can be legitmately added via a scneario for days that are not covered today"

Owner correction, verbatim, to the coordinator's restatement "Where the simulator can't produce something, such as a fuel removal, the scenario can supply it directly":

"this is not true, these are injected through operational actions"

So events such as a fuel removal, a delivery or a hand dip are not written into the scenario as direct data; they enter through operational actions, the same way real operations would.

Coordinator reading (first version, corrected above), passed to the Kisiwa Architect: authored scenario days for periods not covered today are legitimate when they are physically reasonable and reflect a real operating situation (including "after" nights that show an action working). The limit that still applies is the standing no-tuning rule: assumptions are stated up front and not chosen to hit a declared pass mark after seeing a result.

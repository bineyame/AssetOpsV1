The frozen run identity carries Foundation revision ID and content SHA, binding revision ID, scenario ID and SHA, profile IDs/versions and observation SHA, seed and requested timing; it does not embed the thirteen Foundation property values or cold document contents. At execution the host re-reads the exact frozen Foundation revision and cold scenario document, verifies the Foundation revision ID and content SHA and the document's `scenario_sha256`, and recomputes `observation_sha256` from that document. It extracts the thirteen values from that verified Foundation revision and uses the verified document for forcings and observation rows. Missing, changed or mismatched inputs refuse execution before any public Stage output; no current-revision or fixture-default substitution is permitted.

**Cold pack transition closure.** `GENERATOR_FAILURE` is a latent private start fault: the outage still produces `START_REQUEST`, the generator enters `STARTING`, and at the effective Foundation start timeout it becomes `FAILED` and emits `START_RESULT/FAILED` (five minutes, 19:05, in the failure fixtures). On grid restore, the transfer returns to `GRID` immediately and emits `TRANSFER_RESULT/SUCCEEDED` when the configured transfer position sensor is present; the generator stops. While the compressor is `RUNNING` on a powered bus, its published power in kW equals the effective `cold-cooling-capacity` value; otherwise published power is zero. Public grid power and transfer events for one outage use its outage correlation ID; door events use their separate door-cycle correlation. The registered ambient probe publishes an ambient temperature point every five minutes, including the closing boundary, through its bound channel. These are public observations of the resulting transitions, not reports of the private forcing.

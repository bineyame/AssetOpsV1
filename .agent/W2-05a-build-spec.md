## Architect dry-read resolution (2026-10-18; governs any earlier ambiguous wording)

**QA-QD amendment (2026-10-18; governs the W2-05a commit-1 handoff).**

- **QA, timezone:** Use `Etc/UTC` as the EV-LAB-001 Site timezone and as both schedule `time_zone` values, with `utc_offset_minutes=0`. It is an IANA Area/Location name accepted by the shared Site parser. Do not widen that parser for bare `UTC`; replace the earlier fixture wording `UTC` wherever it denotes a Site or schedule zone. UTC timestamps and offset-zero semantics are unchanged.
- **QB, property kinds:** `ev-charger-max-power`, `ev-connector-max-power`, `ev-meter-resolution`, `ev-meter-error-bound` and `ev-clock-error-bound` are `PHYSICAL`. `ev-site-import-limit`, `ev-status-cadence` and `ev-meter-cadence` are `CONTROL`. `ev-connector-enabled` remains `CONTROL`. These are definition kinds, independent of whether a value is currently observed.
- **QC, absent feed:** A Site with no `AC_BUS` may publish a declared charger, meter and connector as an incomplete configuration, provided all declared charger/connector properties and schedule entries are valid. More than one parent for a meter, charger or connector always refuses publication. Once a feed is declared, every declared meter must have exactly that feed as its parent and every declared charger must have exactly one path through a meter from that feed; an unfed meter or charger refuses `EV_CONFIGURATION_INVALID`. Any nonempty connector service schedule requires a complete feed→meter→charger→connector path, even when the Site has no feed.
- **QD, feed cardinality:** Refuse a second `AC_BUS` in an EV Foundation with `EV_CONFIGURATION_INVALID` and a path to the extra feed. The selected response has one `feed`, and no arbitrary feed selection or merged limit is valid. Zero or one feed is publishable subject to the rules above; Draft still requires the feed and its limit.

Build in reviewable commits: **1 vocabulary, parser, typed schedule and validator; 2 EV roles, closed v4 channel matrix, binding publication/snapshot and fixture; 3 selected API; 4 three UI routes and visual proof; 5 migration and final guards/tests.** Commit 5 may be prepared only after W2-04 `0023` is merged into this branch. No interim EV migration is permitted; `0024_w2_ev_config` is reserved for 05a. Run each commit's focused checks and hand off before the next builder run.

**Q1, connector identity.** `BINDING_CHANNEL_OVERLAP` and `EV_CONNECTOR_MISMATCH` stay unchanged. In EV-LAB-001 the c1 tuples are `(c1-charger-controller,session-event)`, `(c1-connector-controller,fault-event|state|requested-power|allocated-limit)`, `(c1-connector-meter,energy|power)`; c2 uses the identical signal names with `c2-` device prefixes. Each of these three devices and seven channels per connector has `component_id=c1` or `c2` respectively and binds to that same EV_CONNECTOR asset. `(site-meter,import)` has `component_id=site-meter-1`, binds to its METER asset, and is the sole Site import channel. `charger-1` is topology and ratings only; it owns no connector channel. Each tuple is a distinct exact entry in `V4_CHANNEL_MAPPINGS`; no prefix wildcard or runtime-generated accepted tuple. Keep the two old unsuffixed W2-00 reserved tuples for their existing tests, label their roles, but do not bind them in this fixture. The v4 snapshot metadata must come from the exact entry. S2's existing one-binding connector check continues to compare the envelope connector asset ID to c1 or c2.

**Q2, readiness.** `CONFIGURATION_INCOMPLETE` applies to `CONNECTOR_NOT_DECLARED`, `FEED_OR_LIMIT_MISSING`, `METER_NOT_BOUND`, `SESSION_EVENTS_NOT_BOUND`, or `STATUS_NOT_BOUND`; `SITE_IMPORT_NOT_BOUND` is a nonblocking capability message under `READY_TO_REPORT`, as are `SOURCE_NOT_CONTACTED` and `COMMISSIONING_UNCONFIRMED`. Missing optional fault, connector power, requested power or allocated limit yields no readiness code; the exact capability that needs it stays unavailable. Required energy, event and state bindings are per declared connector. A configured Site may be ready while source contact and commissioning remain unknown.

**Q3, absent properties.** Missing `ev-site-import-limit` on an otherwise valid feed is the sole publishable numeric gap and emits `FEED_OR_LIMIT_MISSING`; absence of feed or all connectors is likewise publishable as configuration-incomplete so the selected read can explain it. For every *declared* charger/connector, missing `ev-charger-max-power`, `ev-connector-max-power`, `ev-connector-enabled`, `ev-meter-resolution`, `ev-meter-error-bound`, `ev-status-cadence`, `ev-meter-cadence` or `ev-clock-error-bound` refuses Foundation publication with `EV_CONFIGURATION_INVALID` and a path to that property. Present wrong type/unit, nonfinite/out-of-range value, invalid topology or schedule uses the same typed refusal. Do not pretend a missing physical or clock parameter is a readiness finding. A Site with no declared connector cannot publish a binding set or Draft a Lab run.

**Q4, cadence.** `ev-status-cadence` on each connector converts min to seconds and must be `0 < value <= 1`; it is the published state-channel cadence. `ev-meter-cadence` converts min to seconds and must be `0 < value <= 5`; it governs connector energy, connector power, Site import, requested power and allocated limit, all of which publish aligned 300-second *maximum* slots for this profile; the fixture declares exactly 5 min. Event channels have null cadence. `BindingRequest` has no cadence field: derive `bindings[].cadence_seconds` only from the selected Foundation property. Reject a property above its ceiling at Foundation publication (`EV_CONFIGURATION_INVALID`); reject any future submitted cadence field as `BINDING_REQUEST_INVALID`, not an invented `BINDING_CADENCE_INVALID`. `ev-clock-error-bound` is positive seconds and is recorded as the selected configured tolerance; 05a makes no observed clock-attestation claim. No cadence is inferred from observed timestamps.

**Q5, schedule.** Add `connector_enable_schedules` to `foundation_parsing.py:FOUNDATION_CONTENT_KEYS` and both parser allowlists through that shared constant; it is not `site_controls`. Exactly one schedule entry per declared connector, including a disabled connector (which has an empty `weekly` array); an enabled connector may have an empty weekly array, meaning no service hours. Each entry's zone equals the Site's IANA `timezone`. The fixture zone is `Etc/UTC`, weekly TUESDAY 08:00–16:00 with offset 0 for both c1/c2, so it recurs on Tuesdays during the revision's effective period, not just 6 October. For a bounded effective interval, enumerate each occurrence and validate offset and real, unambiguous local endpoints throughout that interval. For an open-ended interval, permit only zones whose offset is fixed for all future occurrences (`Etc/UTC` qualifies); a DST zone needs a bounded revision and a successor when its offset changes. Reject overlap, invalid endpoint and missing entry as `EV_CONFIGURATION_INVALID`. At selected `at`, the effective revision supplies its own schedule; never merge schedules across revisions.

**Q6, fixtures.** 05a creates `backend/tests/fixtures/w2/ev-lab-001.yaml` and registers the Site in its focused test/setup path. There is one Site, `EV-LAB-001`: the stocked Foundation and published binding revision are its first complete basis. Missing meter/event/status examples are separate *test-created binding revisions* on that same Site, with nonoverlapping effective periods after the 6 October complete-run window; the missing-limit example is a later Foundation revision on the same Site. Tests use isolated stores/DBs and pin `at` and revision IDs, so 05b's complete 6 October basis is untouched. No new Site codes are introduced. A revision never rewrites the original. 05b adds scenarios and uses this fixture, not a second copy.

**Q7, migration order.** W2-04 owns `0023`; 05a reserves `0024_w2_ev_config` now. Vocabulary/API/UI commits may proceed before 0023, but do not generate or test 0024 against 0022. Merge reviewed 0023 into the 05a branch, create `0024` with `down_revision` pointing to 0023, then run upgrade/downgrade and final tests. 05b adds no migration.

**Q8, kinds and profile.** `ev_connector_energy` has role `value_kind=INTERVAL_DELTA`, source/canonical unit kWh, parameter-free `IDENTITY_KWH` that accepts only kWh. Other EV interval roles use `value_kind=NUMBER`, `IDENTITY`, kW and v4 `INTERVAL_MEAN`; STATE uses `ENUM`, and EVENT uses `IDENTITY`. `(cX-connector-meter,energy)` requires nonnull session and connector in v4; power and controller interval channels require connector and carry session when a session exists; Site import has explicit-null session/connector. Preserve existing `V4_MAPPING_PROFILE_ID=simulator-lab-gateway-cold` for cold and its historical snapshots; EV binds under the new exact `simulator-lab-gateway-ev/1` profile, with an explicit profile-to-closed-table lookup. Do not mutate cold profile/version or v3 bytes. 05b registers the producer for that EV profile.

# S2-01c phase-2 decisions — Codex Architect, 2026-10-09

These rulings answer `.agent/S2-01c-questions-2.md`; they are not a review of phase 2. The material amendment is `D-2026-10-09-s2-publication-clock-post-run-release` in `.ai/DECISIONS.md`, carried into the S2-01c and S2-02 packets.

1. **Q1 — Accept (b), profile opt-in.** The clock decision governs a registered run *using* the virtual-publication feature; S2-01c AC4 also preserves S2-01b profiles and old sealed bytes. Keep `RUN_VIRTUAL` on the new immutable registered-Kobo profile, leave existing `GATEWAY_UTC` profiles and their delay refusal intact, and make phase-3 setup/UI show which clock the frozen selection uses.

2. **Q2 — Accept (a), no explicit lead while the world executes.** This bounds the operator clock by an actually reached simulation instant so later ordinary readings do not acquire a delay absent from the frozen scenario. Keep `PUBLICATION_AHEAD_OF_WORLD` and its visible refusal in phase 3; after world completion, explicit advancement beyond the end remains allowed without a kernel step.

3. **Q3 — Overrule (a); choose a narrow form of (b).** An advance beyond completion that cannot release any pending report leaves the post-run half of AC4 unproven, whereas a reporting-path publication window can outlast world execution without extending or changing the world or its source samples. Phase 3 must allow a `RUN_VIRTUAL` registered reporting-path delay WINDOW beginning inside the run to end at most one timestep after the world interval, while all forcing, other scenario entries, other profiles and larger overruns retain `INTERVAL_INVALID`; amend the `outside-the-interval` dispatch rule and setup validator to say this explicitly. Preserve the shipped scenario v1 and historical runs, add a new selectable version/fixture whose final in-interval sample is still PENDING at world completion and releases only after explicit post-end advancement, and update its manifest/fingerprints/reviewer proof; source time and value stay fixed, actual gateway `published_at` is the later virtual admission instant, and no kernel step or early V3 sequence occurs.

4. **Q4 — Accept the durable Lab-private schedule.** Its persisted public text, stable key and due instant implement the decision's restart-safe pending report without transferring a delay cause or Lab oracle into V3, Batch V2 or diagnostics. Phase 3 may expose a bounded Lab-only read of pending/released schedule state with source, scheduled and actual publication instants and their clock labels; an absent/unreadable schedule is explicit rather than an invented empty queue.

5. **Q5 — Accept with a bounded meaning.** The persisted delayed-report text lets a restarted host release due reports without rerunning the world; it does not establish that an undelayed series missed during an earlier failed step was regenerated. Phase 3 must distinguish schedule release from complete Stage/central delivery, keep any unadmitted or held work visible where known, and refuse if the required schedule or frozen registered basis is unavailable rather than reconstructing guessed bytes. Prove a host restart while the new post-run report is pending, then advance and compare its identity and bytes across retry.

Phase 3 therefore owns the narrow setup/dispatch-contract amendment and new versioned scenario before UI closeout, plus Lab advancement, schedule and diagnostics presentation, F2/F3 and the packet's full review checks. S2-02 only needs the source-time-valid post-run publication interpretation recorded in its outline; it does not gain Lab-private schedule fields. No owner choice is needed: this is a bounded technical correction to make the already-authorized delayed-release acceptance observable. No product code was changed here, and all edits remain uncommitted.

# Architect rulings: S4-02b `fuel-cost-1` upper bound (2026-10-13)

Branch `plan/S4-02-refine`. These rulings answer Q1 in `.agent/planner-S4-02-questions-2026-10-13.md` and C4 in `.agent/S4-02-plan-review.md` together. They refine `D-2026-10-13-s4-bounded-financial-basis`; the addendum below makes the conditional bound and containment rule explicit.

## Q1 — model multiplier

**Ruling: A, conditionally.** Ratify the exact Decimal, dimensionless `sfc_upper_multiplier = 1.10` as an immutable parameter of the separately reviewed `fuel-cost-1` version. This is an *assumption of that model*: actual consumption per kWh is assumed no greater than `1.10 ×` the selected time-valid Foundation `specific-fuel-consumption` value. It is not a calibrated uncertainty interval, measured maximum, or imported `dispatch-2` constant. The numerical upper is a bound **only under this stated assumption and the C4 input rule below**. Do not show a positive upper before the parameter version and the input rule have independent review. If either fails, publish `QUANTITY_UNAVAILABLE` with null amount; do not silently substitute a point-rate or `SCENARIO` value.

The parameter version must record its exact Decimal value and unitless type; the inequality it asserts and its limitation; `fuel-cost-1` formula/version and immutable parameter ID or digest; the reviewed fixture/model-contract source, author/reviewer and review date or record; applicable Site/generator/period or explicit model scope; and the exact Foundation SFC revision/binding selection rule. Store the parameter version/ID, value, selected SFC revision IDs, accepted power interval IDs and canonical input digest on the consequence lineage. Name the 1.10 choice as a reviewed modelling allowance, **not** as empirical calibration; a later calibrated or Site-specific value needs a new version and review. It cannot be changed in place to reprice a pinned consequence.

**Exact S4-02b packet formula and wording:** `quantity_low = 0 L`; `quantity_high = 1.10 × Σ_j (P_j,mean [kW] × Δt_j [h] × SFC_j [L/kWh])`, over the distinct eligible accepted power intervals defined below, using unrounded Decimal values. The amount bound, when one compatible Site price covers the entire FindingRevision consequence window, is `amount_low = 0` and `amount_high = quantity_high × price`, before outward display rounding. In the default UI use plain wording such as **“Modelled potential fuel-cost exposure: 0 to [amount], under the reviewed fuel-cost-1 assumptions. This is not measured fuel saved or a physical maximum.”** Put `fuel-cost-1`, multiplier, IDs, digest and the full assumption in details/lineage. The owner may settle final product copy at the planned S4-02 checkpoint; no new owner architecture choice is needed now.

## C4 — interval containment and duration

**Ruling: (i), fail-closed containment, with interval-once arithmetic.** `T_i` is the exact `[start,end)` duration of each stored `CANDIDATE` segment with `observed: true`; there is no `T_i,high`. Never use dispatch interval `upper_minutes`, `off_before` or `off_after` as Candidate duration. Build the union `C` of those Candidate spans for the exact selected FindingRevision, keeping generator asset identity. For a generator asset, select the complete accepted, good, nonnegative `GEN_POWER` mean intervals that intersect `C`. Each selected interval `[a_j,b_j)` must lie wholly within `C` for that asset, and their union must cover `C` without a gap. Distinct selected intervals must not overlap in time for the same asset; refuse ambiguous duplicate or conflicting readings rather than choosing or counting twice. The selected, time-valid Foundation SFC must be a single positive L/kWh value throughout each power interval, with unambiguous revision and binding provenance. If any condition fails for any Candidate span, the **whole Finding consequence** is `QUANTITY_UNAVAILABLE`, not a partial amount. A straddling interval that extends into Necessary, Indeterminate, unobserved or outside-Finding time fails even if its mean is accepted. Adjacent Candidate segments may form one contiguous span; an interval crossing their shared edge is eligible and is counted once.

**Exact S4-02b packet formula and wording:** For each eligible distinct interval `j`, set `Δt_j = (b_j − a_j)` in hours. Use `E_j = P_j,mean × Δt_j` as the accepted mean's full-interval kWh, then `quantity_high = Σ_j (E_j × SFC_j × 1.10) L` and `quantity_low = 0 L`. The containment test ensures the full-interval energy is only from observed Candidate time. Do not compute `max(mean) × segment duration`: a mean can hide a higher-power part on one side of a segment cut. Do not split a straddling interval by assuming its mean applies uniformly to a subinterval. Store each counted interval once with its power value, duration, SFC revision, Candidate spans covered and overlap/containment result. This formula is a conditional model upper for potentially avoidable fuel; it makes no counterfactual saving claim.

**Unavailable fixtures S4-02b must add or retain:** missing accepted power; a positive-duration observed Candidate span with a coverage gap; a good power interval crossing a Candidate→Necessary or Candidate→Indeterminate edge (including the case where its mean times Candidate duration would understate energy); an interval extending into unobserved or outside-Finding time; overlapping or conflicting accepted power intervals; and missing, nonpositive, conflicting or mid-interval changing SFC/binding. Include a positive fixture in which one good interval crosses **only** the shared edge of two contiguous observed Candidate segments and is counted once. Also fixture an open dispatch interval with `upper_minutes = null` and complete contained Candidate power coverage: it remains quantifiable from stored Candidate endpoints, proving that `upper_minutes` is never used. Assert unavailable fixtures have no numeric quantity high or amount, and positive fixtures recompute from exact accepted IDs after reload.

The owner should see the bounded and straddling-unavailable cases in the scheduled S4-02 walkthrough and approve the visible explanation there. No separate owner decision is needed for this conservative source rule; it implements the already selected conditional financial basis.

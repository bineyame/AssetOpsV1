## Window and frozen plan

Use `reserve-trial-verification-1`, version 1. The Action must be APPLIED with a committed change artifact. Freeze an immutable VerificationPlanRevision before the later run or its first accepted report. It pins Action, origin FindingRevision, applied event, old and new FoundationRevision, baseline selected S3 assessment/run and accepted evidence IDs, model/cohort/clock basis, target, guardrails, coverage rule and explicit two-window selection. A new plan revision can replace a pending plan only before later evidence is accepted; after that, a new attempt needs a new plan revision and later window. Never edit a plan or silently substitute a newer Finding/assessment.

The baseline is a closed, assessed, half-open 60-minute UTC window wholly before the applied revision's `effective_from`, under the old covering FoundationRevision. The later window is another closed, half-open 60-minute UTC window wholly at or after that effective time, under the new covering revision. Neither window may straddle a policy change. The plan fixes their UTC bounds before the later run; both have the same duration and clock hour, and cite comparable Site, asset, dispatch model version, signal roles and cohort rule. Differences in load/PV or other operating conditions are displayed as limitations; if they prevent a like-for-like comparison under the plan's predeclared comparable-input bands, return INCONCLUSIVE. This is an observed policy-trial comparison, not a causal saving. The S3 `CANDIDATE_AVOIDABLE` duration in minutes from the selected 60-minute dispatch assessment is the target metric; do not substitute all generator run time, a money amount or a Finding count. Baseline must have positive supported Candidate minutes. Version 1 target is at least a 25% reduction in Candidate minutes (`later <= 0.75 * baseline`), with the exact integer/decimal comparison and bounds stored in the plan. Both windows require final S3 selection and complete S4 episode reassessment for the selected Finding. A later `NO_LONGER_SUPPORTED` FindingRevision can corroborate but cannot replace the numeric window comparison.

Minimum coverage is every required five-minute slot in both windows for generator state/power, load, PV, battery SOC and effective reserve; require the dispatch capability inputs needed for Candidate classification, accepted clock/cohort proof and no material rejected/overlapping/contradictory span. A missing slot or required role is insufficient, even if the remaining slots show improvement. Service guardrail requires accepted requested and delivered critical-load power for every slot, or a separately reviewed equivalent service-availability signal; compare unserved critical energy over both windows and require later <= baseline. Reserve guardrail requires accepted SOC and time-valid reserve throughout, with zero post-change minutes below the effective reserve and no increase over baseline. Terminal SOC at identical horizon must be at least baseline terminal SOC after a predeclared comparable initial-SOC band; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE. A service or reserve limit that cannot be measured remains UNKNOWN, never pass. The Planner must version the five-minute publication profile and readiness/missing-input messages for both Lab and real-Site onboarding; no private simulator state fills public gaps.

## Verdict, history and hold

Only `VERIFIED`, `INEFFECTIVE`, `INCONCLUSIVE` are outcome values. Before a closed, settled later window, display `PENDING` as verification state, not an outcome. On a fully assessable pair, `VERIFIED` requires the target and every guardrail to pass. `INEFFECTIVE` means the target fails or any measured guardrail fails. Store `GUARDRAIL_FAIL` as a reason with each failed metric, value, threshold and evidence IDs; it is not a fourth outcome. If a required target, guardrail, source designation, comparability or selection is unknown/ambiguous, return `INCONCLUSIVE`, even when another measured metric failed; retain the known failure as a finding within the outcome, never imply success. A positive verdict requires one coherent stored source designation for each window (`REGISTERED_SIMULATION` for the demo, or `AUTHORED_DEMONSTRATION` for an explicitly authored case) and a compatible pair declared in the plan. `MIXED`, `UNKNOWN`, broken links, or crossing source classes fail closed to INCONCLUSIVE. No real-source positive verdict until separately attested real provenance exists. Label simulated/authored results and make no realized-saving claim.

Late accepted readings, changed clock attestation, source classification, or pending/failed S3 or S4 recomputation immediately mark the latest verdict `REASSESSMENT_PENDING` (a read state, not a fourth outcome) and withdraw its current Verified/Resolved presentation. Keep the old outcome addressable as historical. Once settled, append a new immutable outcome referencing exact selected assessment, FindingRevision, accepted SourceEnvelope IDs/digests, provenance resolution, plan and comparison values; supersede by link, never UPDATE. Identical evaluation retries return the same outcome; a changed basis yields a new outcome. Exact plan/outcome ID and recorded cutoff reads remain stable. A later recurring Finding is a new episode/link, not a rewrite of the prior outcome or proof that the old trial never happened.

Application and verification states remain separate. An APPLIED Action is unresolved while PENDING, REASSESSMENT_PENDING or INCONCLUSIVE. A final current VERIFIED or INEFFECTIVE outcome appends a `RESOLVED` ActionEvent (with outcome ID, plan ID and result) and closes that trial for the prior-Action acceptance guard; INEFFECTIVE means the trial is resolved as unsuccessful, not the Finding repaired. A new reserve trial still needs a fresh current supported FindingRevision and current Foundation basis. A pending or failed application has no verification plan and keeps the hold; a future explicit cancellation/abandonment contract is outside S6-02. If late evidence invalidates the final basis, append a `RESOLUTION_WITHDRAWN` event, restore the hold under the same Site/Finding/asset/field/rule serialization used by accept, and block a new accept while reassessment is pending. If a subsequent Action already exists, do not delete it; flag the overlap and prevent another acceptance until reviewed. The guard and current outcome must be checked atomically, not from a stale UI projection.

The evaluation is system-computed from accepted public evidence. A server-authorized SYSTEM demonstration command may freeze the plan, request evaluation or append the resolution event, with stable service/job and fixture provenance; it cannot supply a verdict or metric value. No gateway credential, client text or local user is a human author. Show the SYSTEM demonstration label. Each plan, evaluation request, outcome and ActionEvent is append-only with server recorded time and idempotency key; retries and exact-history selectors follow S6-01's recorded-order rule.

## Demo and delivery split

`I-sim-verification-cases` produces two separate fixed-policy Lab runs, old then new Foundation, through registered gateway and ordinary S2 acceptance. A replayable manifest cites public run/batch/envelope IDs, plan and expected selected S3/S4 revisions; private oracle stays in Lab. Include a complete target-and-guardrails pass, a complete target pass with a measured reserve or service guardrail failure (`INEFFECTIVE`, `GUARDRAIL_FAIL`), and missing service/terminal or contradictory/late evidence (`INCONCLUSIVE`, then reassessment and an appended settled result). Include a target miss, closed-window zero-finding basis and prior-Action hold/release proof. The examples say simulated policy trial and no causal or savings claim.

Build non-UI first: immutable plan/outcome persistence and Action events, rule evaluator, exact read APIs, source and pending-work gates, fixtures, replay/idempotency/race tests. Then UI: Recommendation plan/evidence, Action Plan/Evidence/Verification, `changes:1#b` and `changes:2`, with exact IDs, activity separate from effect, missing/pending/fail states at desktop and 390 px. S6-02 owner walkthrough remains required. S6-03 Site ActionIndex is independent.

Conversion potential: what feeds the four Portfolio cards, and what data would make them sell

Part 1 traces each of the four Portfolio cards from the reading to the number on screen, for the mini-grid, cold-chain and EV Sites, exactly as the code does it. Part 2 sets that against what operators, finance teams and investors in African markets are judged on, and the data each card would need. It closes with the demo moments most likely to convert, a script for the data conversation, and ranked build proposals.

In plain words
  1. Two of the four cards are carried by one Site type each. Optimize gets money only from the mini-grid generator finding. Protect's headline count can only come from cold-chain and EV findings. Cold chain adds no money to any card, and EV adds money only to Grow.
  2. Every money figure is an estimate built from your declared prices. None comes from a bill, a meter account or a ledger. The code marks each one honestly ("not billed or collected", "not a saving", "loss not priced"). Those labels are also exactly where a CFO or investor stops trusting the number.
  3. The things investors judge first don't exist in the product yet: revenue per customer, cash collected, uptime against demand, cost per kWh delivered, and paid cold-room occupancy. The catalogue already names most of them as "not connected yet" (MG-6, MG-3, CC-2, CC-4, EV-6, EV-2). The gap is the data, not the design.
  4. All five suspected gaps, (a) to (e), are confirmed, with nuances in section 7. I found three more. CC-3 "Cost per cooling unit" is flagged as measured although it has no cost input, so its row reads "No objective declared" instead of "Not measured yet". EV "idle chargers" is mapped but nothing produces it, although the mock's Grow card names it. Verified results are counted from fix status, not from any outcome.
  5. The quickest gain is a billing/payments + customer-meter feed for mini-grids, through CSV first and an API later. It turns MG-6 and MG-3 on, gives Grow a collected figure next to the estimate, and gives the per-kWh card a real denominator. Part 2 ranks this and the other gaps.

1. How a reading becomes a card

Every card follows the same seven-step chain. Two steps carry most of the meaning. The outcome map decides which outcome a signal "moves" (its first entry is the primary) and which outcomes it is only "checked" against. The contribution records decide whether that outcome gets a target, an earnings figure or a potential, and which pricing rule (strategy) makes the money.

The rule that decides whether a row can say anything at all

Before any signal counts, each outcome passes three gates in order (status.py:67-72). It must apply to the Site. It must be measured: a catalogue constant, so MG-6, MG-3, CC-2, CC-4, EV-6 and EV-2 always read "Not measured yet · … not connected yet". And it must be settled: the composer has read its inputs for the window. Only then do open findings, warnings and declared objectives set Needs attention, Watch or On track. This is why a missing data source shows up as an honest grey row and not as a zero.

Settled maps: mini site_outcomes/mini.py:596-603; cold site_outcomes/cold.py:164-168; EV site_outcomes/ev.py:171-174. Row not-measured sentence: catalogue.py:72-83, board.py:895-900.

Flexible
  • Prices are declared assumptions with revisions, so a client can enter its own tariff and every figure reprices.
  • A new income source needs one CONTRIBUTIONS record with node earnings and a label in INCOME_LABELS. The card then sums it without a code change.
  • The "At least" rule and per-Site lines already handle partial days, mixed currencies and unreadable Sites.
Hard-wired limits
  • Income is energy × declared price. There is no billed or collected amount anywhere. recorded_amount@1 always returns "no records".
  • Only two income labels are summed. A third (cold-room fees, say) also needs its vocabulary label.
  • Potential exists for mini headroom only. EV idle capacity is mapped, but nothing produces it.
  • Mini income covers sub-metered productive users only, not household or commercial customers, so it understates site revenue by design.

What a new data source would take. Billing/payments for mini-grids: a store (customer, meter, invoice, payment), an adapter (CSV upload, then a SparkMeter/Steama-style API), a chain measure such as collected_amount, a record moving MG-6 with a recorded_amount-style strategy that finally returns money, and flipping MG-6 to measured in the catalogue. The card then needs a deliberate ruling on whether "collected" is shown beside "estimated" or replaces it. They should never be added together.

3. Optimize cost and efficiency

What the card shows. When one Site is priced: that Site's claim text, e.g. "120–180 USD possible extra fuel cost", with "At {Site} · not added across sites". When several are priced: "Costs at N sites" and a line for each. When none: "Not known yet · A cost needs a priced record". Basis: "Modelled from the stored record and the declared price · not a saving".

Flexible
  • The fuel model is versioned (cost-1, cost-2), and parameters are declared per generator (SFC, no-load rate).
  • The @COST alias already routes "repeat visit, no result" in all three verticals. Price that one input (call-out cost) and all three light up.

Hard-wired limits
  • One Finding mechanism is the only source of money. No opex ledger, fuel invoice, repair cost or energy bill exists.
  • The card shows a possible extra cost range starting at 0. It is not a unit cost (USD/kWh) and not a saving.

Maintenance cost: the accepted "Price it" design already covers this with a declared repair_visit_cost per visit (and planned_service_cost, charger_repair_cost). These keys are listed on the Site price page (financials/price_page.py:58-87) but are not storable until the PR-3 key migration lands; PR-4 recalculates on publish. Cost columns on maintenance_record / work_case are a later step, for real invoices. Odometer: a new telematics adapter plus an EV-2 measure.

Flexible
  • "Disrupting" is a property of the map: any future mechanism whose first moved outcome is Protect counts automatically.
  • Early warnings attach to any outcome through the map, and the detail line counts them per category.

7. The five suspected disconnects, and three more

(a) confirmed
The mini-grid can never add to "sites disrupted". The count takes threads whose first moved outcome is Protect (claims.py:151-161). Mini-grids have two Finding mechanisms. GENERATOR_SUPPORT_CANDIDATE moves MG-2 (Optimize) first. UNEXPLAINED_FUEL moves MG-8 (Protect) but sits in NOT_DISRUPTION_MECHANISMS (claims.py:43). Warnings are not threads, and the headroom opportunity has no Finding occurrence, so neither counts (claims.py:124-134). Nuance: the mini-grid still reaches the card's detail line (battery and plan warnings) and the fuel lines. The mock's "2 sites disrupted" is also cold plus EV only (v70/outcomes.js:191), so this matches the mock. It is a product gap, not a build defect.
(b) confirmed
Assure counts something the outcome map does not route. The map sends only EVIDENCE_GAP and EVIDENCE_READINESS to Assure (MG-7/CC-7/EV-7, outcome_map.py:26-27, 40). The card value is verified_n = verified threads of any category plus verified opportunity actions (claims.py:210), and the detail counts targets on any outcome (read.py:944-951). No catalogue outcome exists for "verified result" or "target met" (catalogue.py:43-68). So the Assure category has two meanings: evidence quality on the Site board, and proven fixes on the Portfolio.
(c) confirmed
MG-5 unserved energy is a board reading only. critical_served is a chain measure (chain/measures.py) that yields "x kWh not served over the whole day" (mini.py:846-853). It is used only to judge a declared MG-5 objective. MG-5 has no contribution record ("MG-5 has none (R9)", outcome_map.py:149), no Finding moves it, and it is never priced. Nuance: a missed MG-5 target does turn the Site's Protect row to Needs attention (status.py:79-80), and its days count in the coverage line. It still never reaches the card count.
(d) confirmed
MG-2 "Supply cost per kWh" has no actual unit cost. Its target is avoidable generator minutes (outcome_map.py:161-162). Its money is a modelled 0–high fuel range (:163-166). Nothing divides total supply cost by kWh delivered: there is no fuel spend (delivery tickets hold litres only, migration 0054), no opex, no capex. Nuance: a verified trial's stored cost check can say "x USD less supply cost per kWh" (board.py:60-61, 549-566). That is a modelled change for one trial hour, not a level.
(e) confirmed
Six outcomes are "not connected yet". MG-6, MG-3, CC-2, CC-4, EV-6 and EV-2 are created with a missing_records sentence, which sets measured = False (catalogue.py:38-39, 45-46, 54-56, 61-62). They always read Not measured, and no table or adapter exists for billing, payments, customers, occupancy, contracts, produce prices or odometers (checked across all migrations). Nuance: cold Grow and EV Optimize are therefore whole rows that can never change state.
(f) new
CC-3 "Cost per cooling unit" is flagged measured but has no cost input. It is moved only by the repeat-visit warning (@COST). The row's own contributors say "Repair and call-out costs are not connected yet" and "no cooling energy meter" (cold.py:71-72). Because it is measured=True (catalogue.py:63), the row reads "No objective declared" when no warning fires, and Needs attention or Watch when one does. It cannot read On track, because no declarable objective measure exists for CC-3 (outcomes/objectives.py:57-72, status.py:88-102). This is a small honesty issue: the row should read "Not measured yet". The same applies in a softer form to MG-2 (d).
(g) new
"Idle chargers at Depot" is in the mock's Grow card but has no producer. The mock text is "Productive use at Kobo · idle chargers at Depot" (v70/outcomes.js:189). WARNING:CHARGERS_MOSTLY_IDLE is mapped with a record (outcome_map.py:36, 177-180), but outcomes/warnings.py:29 has no such kind (backlog BL-06). The EV Grow potential therefore always reads "No potential found yet". This is the clearest "data is here, producer is missing" gap: charging kWh and charger ratings already exist.
(h) new
Cold chain adds no money to any card, and EV adds money only to Grow. Of the three verticals, only the mini-grid has a money path into Optimize, and only cold chain and EV can raise Protect. A cold-chain-only prospect would see a Portfolio with grey Grow, an empty Optimize, a red Protect and an Assure saying "no verified result yet". That is honest, but a weak demo for that buyer.

10a. Africa-specific items that shape the data conversation

Currency and FX

Operators bill in NGN, KES or UGX, while investors read USD. Naira volatility is a live CFO topic. Our income total already refuses to add mixed currencies ("Not shown · amounts in more than one currency", vocabulary.py:300). A USD investor view would need a declared FX rate per window, labelled as such, and is not built.

Customer-data consent

Per-customer billing and meter exports are personal data under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 and Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023. Ask for exports aggregated by customer class where possible. Pseudonymised meter IDs remain personal data under both Acts, so they reduce exposure but still need a lawful basis and a data-processing agreement before we receive them. Cross-border transfer rules also apply if data leaves the country (Kenya DPA 2019 ss.48–49; Nigeria DPA 2023 ss.41–43), so hosting location belongs in that agreement.

Non-technical loss

Generated or delivered kWh minus billed kWh is the COO's theft-and-leakage figure. It needs billing (#3), and it is then the strongest follow-on lever. Added as gap #4a.

Mini-grid carbon

Diesel displaced by solar is a carbon revenue line for mini-grids as well as EVs. It needs measured generation and generator kWh (we have these), plus a methodology and baseline. Added as a later gap.

11. Conversion levers: demo moments most likely to convert

Mini-grid
  1. "Your generator ran when the battery could have carried the load." Show the minutes and the modelled fuel range at the client's own diesel price, then enter their price on the Site price page (site-admin entry, PR-3). This puts diesel exposure into the CFO's number.
  2. The verified trial. Before and after, with litres saved in the trial hour. This is evidence-grade proof, which is what REPP-style "independently verified" asks for.
  3. Unexplained fuel. Tank dips and deliveries that do not balance. COOs remember fuel leakage.
  4. Spare daytime solar priced as productive-use income (the mill). This is the ARPU-growth story.
  5. The honest grey rows: "Cash collected · not connected yet". Use it as the bridge into the data request below.
Cold chain
  1. The reconstructed excursion. Power failed, the backup did not start, the chamber rose above 4°C for 25–35 min, someone started the backup by hand. The full causal chain is on one screen.
  2. Backup-start reliability across events, with a witnessed start test as the fix.
  3. Repeat visits without a result. "3 call-outs, fault still there" is a maintenance-contract conversation.
  4. Stock-value what-if: the existing scenario shows the stock quantity and its unit value side by side. It computes no value-at-risk amount. A declared per-load value (stock_value_at_risk), which would give "up to … at risk", is designed but not storable yet.
EV
  1. Interrupted sessions and which connector caused them, with time out of service.
  2. Estimated charging income at their tariff, then re-price the same sessions as two single-tariff scenarios (KSh 8 and KSh 16). A true peak/off-peak split is not built (gap #8).
  3. Peak near the import limit, "room before expansion". This speaks to capex planning.
  4. Session evidence quality: missing ends and status gaps. An MRV-ready dataset is the carbon-credit hook.

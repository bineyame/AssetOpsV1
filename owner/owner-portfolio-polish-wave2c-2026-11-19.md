6. Lower half: columns of unequal height and mostly empty outcome cards read as a grid not designed for the content.
7. East Africa identity is very subtle: the two packs look almost the same at a glance. It needs a stronger but professional expression (illustration, typeface or a characteristic accent), not a border.

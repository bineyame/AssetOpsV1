# Owner direction 2026-11-19 (verbatim): website copy pass

The owner asked:

> is the website content update extremely motivating and highlights the differntiation well without spelling out differntiation

## Coordinator's assessment and proposal (the owner approved it)

**Assessment.** Not yet. The copy is accurate and honest, and it passes the lint.

What works:
- the sharp per-vertical questions;
- the concrete, counter-intuitive examples.

What holds it back:
1. It states the differentiation outright ("Monitoring tells you what your assets did…"), naming and arguing against the competitor category.
2. Honesty labels repeat inside the prose ("Illustration on demo data, Emulated, with simulated telemetry; not a customer result", "being built").
3. "Today" lines describe mechanisms, not value.
4. Defensive framing leads ("We do not promise savings", "never judged", "sends no command").
5. The strongest executive hook is missing from Home: which sites earn, which leak, and where limited budget and crews should go, with the payoff proven.
6. The four outcome categories are not used as the frame.

**Proposal.** A dedicated copy pass with these rules:
- Lead with outcomes.
- Show the differentiation; do not state it.
- Make portfolio allocation and proof the hero.
- Use the four categories as the executive frame.
- Move honesty into one consistent "Demo data" badge, plus the evidence page.
- Keep every existing honesty rule and the lint unchanged; the lint must pass.

The owner answered:

> yes, have the copy pass done

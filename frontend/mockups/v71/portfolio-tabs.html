What these words mean
Complete readings
Readings arrived for at least 80% of the hours the day needed (for spare solar: every daylight hour). The day is counted.
Partial readings
Readings arrived for 25–80% of those hours. The day is shown, and named wherever it is used.
Too few readings
Readings arrived for under 25% of those hours. The day is not counted.
No readings received
Nothing arrived from the site for that day.
Still processing
Readings arrived and are being analysed; results follow.
Not analysed yet
The day has not been through the site analysis yet, whatever readings it has.

AssetOps looks at every day. These words describe the data that arrived, not whether anyone looked.

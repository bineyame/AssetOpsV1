What changes from v6.9, and why

  • "High priority" is removed. AssetOps does not grade findings by priority. "Where management should act first" on the Portfolio (Act first tab) is the ranking, and it has its own reasons. A card that always says "Not assessed" tells the reader nothing.
  • "Low confidence" becomes "Not yet confirmed". There is no per-finding confidence grade on this page. "Not yet confirmed" is a real state every finding already carries, and it answers the same question: which findings should be read with care?
  • "Supported findings" becomes "Confirmed findings", the same word as the state chip on this page.
  • Counts show with an honest qualifier instead of "Some data missing". The qualifier uses the analysis words (fully analysed / days not analysed yet / still processing / no new readings received / not analysed yet), not the reading words, because a site's findings depend on whether its days were analysed.

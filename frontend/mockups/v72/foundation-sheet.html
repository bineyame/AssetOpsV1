The decisions this sheet fixes. Each one follows your item 13 answers.

FontInter, one font for everything

Open licence (OFL 1.1), stored with the app, no outside call. Covers French accents. If it cannot load, the computer's own font shows, numbers still aligned.

ThemesLight and Dark, chosen by the user

Control in the top bar: System, Light, Dark. System is the default: the app follows the computer's setting until the user picks one.

The frame in both themes

The Portfolio frame from v7.1, redrawn in the new tokens: left menu (unchanged), top bar with the shared date range, the language switcher and the theme control, the page heading, the coverage line once, and the Portfolio tab row. The theme control here works too.

Tokens in three tiers

Primitive values have no meaning (a blue, 16 px). Semantic tokens say what a value is for (page background, partial readings text) and have a light and a dark value. Component tokens say how one component uses them (chip height, card corner). Pages use only semantic and component tokens; the style check added in UQ-1 stops new raw colours and sizes. Type

Eight sizes, a 12 px floor and 14 px body. Weights 400, 500, 600, 650/700. Headings are sentence case. Every number in a table, a card figure or a money amount uses tabular figures, so columns line up.

Components in both themes

Each component is drawn from component tokens only. Words are the product's own (the readings term family approved as D-E5.46, the outcome names, current page labels). Figures are illustrative.

French and Swahili lengths

Layout test only. These are rough drafts to check that tabs, chips and buttons survive longer words; the real wording comes from UQ-8, and a language is offered only after its reviewer signs off.

Contrast (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Computed live from the tokens on this page. Text needs 4.5:1 (all text here is under 18.66 px bold or 24 px, so the large-text 3:1 allowance is not used). Control borders and the focus ring need 3:1 against what they sit on. Status chips are checked against their own background.

What changes on the real pages

  1. Text gets bigger. Table text 11 → 13–14 px, table headers 9 → 12 px, menu group names 9 → 12 px, notes 10–11 → 12–13 px, page titles 27 → 24 px. Some long tables get taller, and phones scroll a little more.
  2. One font, Inter, on Windows, Mac and phones alike, with numbers that line up in columns.
  3. A theme control in the top bar (System, Light, Dark), next to the language switcher, beside the shared date range. The choice is kept per browser; the first visit follows the computer.
  4. Every colour comes from one list. The 228 different colours in the app today are replaced by the semantic tokens above, including the charts and drawings, so dark mode reaches every page.
  5. One look per state. "Complete readings", "Partial readings" and the other six reading words always look the same, on every page, in both themes.
  6. Empty panels say why and what next, and a Site-board panel that could only say "nothing" folds into one "Not shown yet, and why" line.
  7. Loading shows grey placeholder blocks in the page's shape instead of "Loading…" text; the frame and menu stay put.
  8. Link text never shows a raw date code such as 2026-11-08: it reads "Open 8 Nov dispatch day".
  9. Not changed: the left menu, page layouts, the words on the pages, "Why?" links and every qualifier. Layout changes arrive only with their own approved mockups (v7.1 Portfolio, Findings cards, shared date range).

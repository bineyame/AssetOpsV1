## 1. The rule and the boundary

### 1.1 Four kinds (D-0K4.31)

Every number in the pack or the mock that was a design value, or was computed from one, becomes one of these:

1. **a declared fact**: a setting, price, quote, load or record of the simulated world;
2. **a v8 study figure**: read from 0K4d's `study.json` through the committed extract (§2.1), labelled SIMULATED, MODELLED,
   with the study's assumptions. Money the pack's evaluator computes from such a figure and declared facts is a
   figure of this kind too, and carries the same label (D-0K4.32);
3. **"Not available yet"**, naming its record or unit;
4. **an admission-rule line**: the result of `runs/admission.py` read from the study, or information beside it, such as the dawn.

A **design value** is a number for a quantity that a model or a record computes, typed in by hand. In the manifest
these are the params marked `design_value: K|M|E`, the `forward_replay` and `economics` rows of replay options, KIS-BATT60's
assumed 150 L, and the 330/730 dispatch split of Kobo's October. "Design estimate" and "design value" disappear as
labels everywhere.

### 1.2 The world view is not edited (D-0K4.35)

The product's world generator reads the manifest's view: `manifest_version`, `as_of`, `accounting_period`, `currency`,
`timezone_default`, `history`, and in `tenants` each site's `site`, `code`, `physical`, `tariff` and `period`
(`plan`, `actual`, `coverage`) (`simulator/assetops_simulator/world/view.py:17-20`). The DS1b records, the 3A3
exports and the 5c walkthrough world are built from that view. **0K4m changes no byte the view reads.** Two design
values sit inside it:

- Kobo's `period.actual` `necessary_l: 730, candidate_l: 330`;
- Mto's `physical.extra_l_per_day_at_half_inverter_capacity: 5` and its `extra_l_basis`.

Both stay in the file and move with 0K4f, which rebuilds the world (D-0K4.13). **(rev 4, D-0K4.45)** Kobo's split is

### 1.3 The world-month rule (rev 2, R2; D-0K4.38)

A study figure reaches the world's month only as a **rate on a quantity the option itself declares**: litres per
customer kWh times the option's declared kWh (MTO-HULLER, MTO-WELDING), or extra litres per day times the option's
days at reduced capacity (the MTO-INV paths). An **amount of a site's existing running** from a replayed season is
never added to or subtracted from the world's record of another month, either as litres or as a share. Kobo's two
settings are that case. K8's avoided litres and added throughput come from a season whose generator burns more a
month than Kobo's recorded October, so both settings' litres, throughput and value read "Not available yet" until the
model runs on Kobo's October as recorded (0K4f). Their admission result and dawn information stay, stated in the
replay's own frame ("of 61 replayed days", "most starts in a day"), never as an amount per month. K12 enforces the
rule. The packet prints each study's season generator litres beside the site's recorded generator litres, as
residual-risk evidence; the screen never shows the pair.

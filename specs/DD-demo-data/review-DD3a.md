### Findings

**M1 (Medium; a W6 class, the same as D-DD.90's seasons): Kobo's July-September history moved by re-seeding, not by its kit.**
- Where: `simulator/assetops_simulator/world/scope.py:45-46` puts the whole `site_doc["physical"]` into every scope
  digest. `history.py:183-187` (`physical`) is read only by the EV rules (`history.py:356,358`). The manifest's
  mini-grid rules read no `physical` key (`grep physical demo-site-scenario-manifest.yaml`: none in Kobo's rules).
- What: commit 2 changed Kobo's `genset_kw`, `sfc_l_per_kwh` and `battery_kwh`. That changed every Kobo scope digest,
  so every seed changed, so every jittered leaf changed. The packet's moved list shows the jitter. Delivered kWh went
  +175, −6 and +224 (Jul, Aug, Sep), and losses went +8, −1 and +10. Customer load has no causal link to the size of
  the generator. Necessary litres rose (711 to 730) while SFC fell (0.30 to 0.2917). That is the opposite direction
  from any physical effect.
- Why it matters: these are hero-Site story figures that clients see: July billed 8163 to 8329, revenue, and
  contribution (Sep 891.45 to 975.40, portfolio 1594.15 to 1678.10). Nothing in the operating world moved them. The
  owner's rule is "one figure, loyal to the operating world". D-DD.90 has already ruled this class for `seasons`: "a
  scope digest covers exactly what its rules read".
- Fix (an Architect ruling, Q1): restrict `site["physical"]` in `scope_input` to the keys that a rule of the scope reads.
  For mini-grid that is none today, and for EV it is `buses` and `kwh_per_bus_night`. Add a parity test with a golden
  from main: every non-Kobo scope digest is unchanged, and Kobo's digests equal main's. Then Kobo's July-September
  `history.json` returns to main's figures. K8, the K13 `history` part and `PINNED_ALL_MONTHS` revert or re-base. The
  packet's moved list shrinks to the `physical` lines themselves. This is not an acceptance gap of DD3a, because D-DD.30
  said to regenerate. It is a model fault the regeneration exposed.

**M2 (Medium; a spec gap, so an Architect question): the readings span to 6 Nov 06:00 has no load rule, and the writer stops at 31 Oct.**
- Where: spec §2 (line 168, "Readings, Kobo: 2026-07-01T00:00 to 2026-11-06T06:00 local") and §4.2 "Load" (October
  `period.actual`, July-September `history.json`, nothing for November). `telemetry.py:325-327` refuses
  `TEL_INPUT_TOTAL <month> has no books` for 2026-11. `books_for` (`telemetry.py:655-688`) builds only the history
  months and the accounting period. `world_history.py:226` defaults `--end` to 31 Oct. Day files are whole local
  days, so a 06:00 end has no form.
- Why it matters: C-DD5 ("readings on every day of the span"), C-DD9's range cells and the IA-P range rule all need
  1-6 Nov for every Site. D-DD.90 adds November's sun and cold ambient, but not mini-grid load. The writer would refuse
  at DD5/DD6, not before.
- Fix: a ruling (Q2). Once it lands, `books_for` and the day-file writer follow it, with a test that a 1 Jul to 6 Nov
  06:00 span is written and that its last file ends at 06:00.

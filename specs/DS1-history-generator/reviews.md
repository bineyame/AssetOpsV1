### M1 (Medium; a spec gap for the Architect, folded into MB5): history sides drop October's assumption entries

- **Where.** `P/evaluate_structure.py:794-797`. (b) applies only `unreceived_leaves` on the actual side, and the plan side
  gets nothing.
- **What.** October's `missing-records.json` labels two kinds of value that rest on an assumption. History carries
  neither label:
  - **Target-side leaves with no plan line.** October's plan side has an `ASSUMPTION` entry (`target_side`: "leaks and
    avoidable use are taken at zero"). It covers Kobo, Kisiwa, Mto and Embakasi, and Ruiru's plan has a `RESIDUAL` entry.
    The history plans are generated with the same key sets, so the same leaves are filled the same way. For example, Kobo
    July plan `nodes` show `technical_loss_kwh: 0.0`, `candidate_l: 0.0` and `unexplained_l: 0.0`, and the plan
    `contribution` that every `gap` point uses rests on them. None of this carries a record.
  - **Site-level assumption.** Embakasi's `tariff.applicability` is "[assumption]" (manifest :604; `empty_states.assumptions`
    :1673 blocks `energy_cost`). The history `energy_cost` (Jul actual 19685.76) uses the same tariff, but Embakasi's
    history sides have `missing: []`.
- **Why it matters.** Under D-DS1.12 and R7, a history value must be no less honest than October's. Today the history
  looks fully observed where October says "assumption". The gap trend is built on these plan figures. `3B7a` will store
  `missing` unchanged, so the gap travels on.
- **Not against the Implementer.** Spec §8.1 step 4(b) says "refactor its per-side part into a helper". The built reading
  (the `sides[1:]` loop only) is defensible, and the plan side is MB5's open question.
- **Fix, if ruled.** Call the same rules on each history pair:
  - the target-side `ASSUMPTION` or `RESIDUAL` entry on the plan side;
  - the `assumptions` entries on the actual side.
  - Deferred-stage entries are optional: `headroom` is not a history node.
  - Coverage `TARGET` and rights conditions do not apply: coverage is all RECORDED, and the view drops entitlements.
- **Effect.** About 10 lines. No series value changes, and H4 is unaffected. An H-check for it is worth adding.

  its plan is decomposed with `max_kw: fixed`.
- **Fix.** Accept it as realistic budgeting, or give Ruiru's plan the demand component as `fixed` (a §5 change, which

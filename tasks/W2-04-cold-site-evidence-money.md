The consequence worker processes an exact settled Finding/episode/assessment basis, accepted IDs, source gate and effective assumption IDs. A per-work advisory lock plus one transaction appends consequence/evidence links and a `SUCCEEDED` attempt; failure appends a `FAILED` attempt and leaves work retryable. No runtime work INSERT, UPDATE or DELETE. One Site/window/room overlap group prevents backup and excursion exposure from being added twice. Pending, stale, contradictory, mixed/unknown source or absent inputs withhold money. Historical cutoff stays labelled historical. A valid missing claim returns `UNKNOWN`, never fabricated zero.

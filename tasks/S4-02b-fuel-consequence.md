## Reviewed quantity contract and fixture

1. Version `fuel-cost-1` independently of `dispatch-3.1`. Read one CURRENT exact FindingRevision, its selected primary **and all** supporting AssessmentRevision/analytic-run IDs, accepted public generator-power means, candidate segment `[start,end)` spans, as-accepted Foundation/binding revisions and the physical `specific-fuel-consumption` (L/kWh). Revalidate input membership and coverage from accepted IDs; a Finding's candidate minutes alone is not a fuel quantity. Do not use Lab private truth, legacy `dispatch-2` fuel outputs, a constant inferred load, interpolated missing intervals, or Indeterminate/Necessary spans.
2. `fuel-cost-1` uses the immutable, dimensionless model parameter `sfc_upper_multiplier = 1.10`, ratified as an expressly conditional assumption that actual L/kWh does not exceed 1.10 × the selected Foundation SFC (Architect Q1, `.agent/architect-S4-02-answers.md`; addendum to `D-2026-10-13-s4-bounded-financial-basis`). It is not a calibrated or measured bound and not the legacy `SFC_UNCERTAINTY`. Its version records value, inequality, scope, source, reviewer/date, formula and immutable ID/digest; a later value needs a new version, never an in-place change. **Input rule (Architect C4, fail-closed containment):** `T_i` is the exact `[start,end)` of each stored `CANDIDATE` segment with `observed: true` (there is no `T_i,high`; never use interval `upper_minutes`, `off_before` or `off_after`). Build the union `C` of those spans for the exact FindingRevision, per generator asset. Select the complete accepted, good, nonnegative `GEN_POWER` mean intervals intersecting `C`; each `[a_j,b_j)` must lie wholly inside contiguous Candidate time for that asset, their union must cover `C` without gaps, and same-asset intervals must not overlap (refuse duplicates/conflicts). Each interval needs one unambiguous positive time-valid SFC throughout. Any failure for any span makes the **whole** consequence `QUANTITY_UNAVAILABLE` with no amount. **Formula:** `quantity_low = 0 L`; `quantity_high = 1.10 × Σ_j (P_j,mean kW × (b_j − a_j) h × SFC_j L/kWh)`, each distinct interval counted once, unrounded Decimal. Never compute `max(mean) × segment duration` or prorate an interval across a segment cut. Store each counted interval once with power, duration, SFC revision, Candidate spans covered and containment result, plus parameter version/ID and canonical input digest. **Fixtures:** positive complete case; positive case with one interval crossing only the shared edge of two contiguous observed Candidate segments (counted once); open dispatch interval with `upper_minutes = null` but complete contained coverage (quantifiable from stored endpoints). Unavailable: missing power; coverage gap; interval crossing Candidate→Necessary and Candidate→Indeterminate (including where mean × Candidate duration would understate energy); interval into unobserved or outside-Finding time; overlapping/conflicting power; missing, nonpositive, conflicting or mid-interval changing SFC/binding. Unavailable fixtures assert no numeric high or amount; positive fixtures recompute from exact accepted IDs after reload. Default plain wording, final copy at the owner checkpoint: "Modelled potential fuel-cost exposure: 0 to [amount], under the reviewed fuel-cost-1 assumptions. This is not measured fuel saved or a physical maximum." Model name, multiplier, IDs and digest go in details/lineage.
3. Price selection requires one compatible Site `diesel_price_per_litre` revision valid for the **entire** consequence window and known at selected recorded cutoff. The consequence window is the FindingRevision `window_start/end`. A price boundary inside that window leaves it unpriced (`PRICE_SPANS_WINDOW`); do not silently partition quantities or use current price for old operation. Unit/currency mismatch, missing/overlapping/conflicting price or Foundation basis yields typed unavailable/unpriced reason, not a guessed amount. A future-effective price leaves yesterday's selected price and consequence current. A backdated correction valid over yesterday's whole window appends a new consequence for the same FindingRevision and quantity/basis. For a partial-window correction, change only consequences whose full-window selected price changes; a correction that makes a previously priced window cross a price boundary appends an `UNPRICED` consequence revision. `STALE/PENDING` compares the price selected **for that consequence window** and the latest selected FindingRevision with the consequence's pinned IDs; supersession of an unrelated future slice alone is not stale.

## Migration, recompute and reads

- New append-only `financial_consequence_revision`: UUID, tenant/Site/Finding/FindingRevision, consequence type `POTENTIAL_FUEL_COST`, revision number, half-open window, overlap group, basis kind `MODELLED_CONDITIONAL`, formula version, quantity low/high/unit, amount low/high/currency nullable, price selection/reason, limitations, created/recorded time, canonical technical-input digest and separate consequence basis digest. FK/unique `(finding_revision_id,consequence_type,revision_no)` and unique idempotent basis digest; indexes for Site/Finding/window. Link tables `financial_consequence_assessment_revision` (primary plus every supporting exact assessment) and `financial_consequence_assumption_revision` (every used exact price/parameter assumption when represented as an assumption); evidence IDs/accepted envelopes resolve through S4-03. Do not mutate any older row or assessment/Finding. A `quantity` reference scalar, if retained for compatibility, is explicitly labelled, never shown as proved saving.
- On assumption publication or changed selected FindingRevision, record transactional dirty/outbox work keyed by affected Site/key/window/selected revision. Recompute transactionally or retry a durable worker; show `STALE/PENDING` while selection has outrun the consequence. Exact retry returns the existing consequence ID; failed retry leaves typed pending/error, not a silent old current amount. A new FindingRevision produces its own linked consequence. Keep old `UNPRICED`/priced revisions inspectable by ID.
- Multiply unrounded compatible Decimal liters × currency/L to store unrounded low/high. Display low rounded downward and high upward to that currency's minor unit (including non-2-decimal currencies); preserve unrounded values for any later permitted total. Never sum unlike currency, period, basis, overlap group, FindingRevision, scenario or operational lines. Expose selected amount and explicit exclusions; no portfolio total.
- `GET /api/sites/{site_code}/findings/{finding_id}/financial-consequences?revision_id=&consequence_revision_id=&after_revision_no=&limit=` (or equivalent child read) returns exact Finding selection, current/historical consequence selection, complete paged history, price/quantity/amount bounds, valid/recorded times, model/parameter and all assessment/assumption/evidence lineage IDs plus typed `UNPRICED`, `QUANTITY_UNAVAILABLE`, `STALE/PENDING`, closed-source and 401/403/404/503 states. Exact bad/foreign IDs never fall back. Old pinned row is labelled historical; Finding History and S4-03 Evidence links preserve the same revision. The Assumptions History view shows exact price revisions and consequence revisions; the current view never silently substitutes a pinned old price.

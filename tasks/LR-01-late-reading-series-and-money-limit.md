## Reviewed contract first — proposed S3 cohort v4

Architect review of this contract precedes implementation. The recommendation is deterministic and fail closed:

1. For each selected `(gateway, source, sequence_epoch, role, asset)` channel, use **source-time order** only when a durable, versioned, Site/source/epoch-scoped clock attestation (new) proves the source clock's maximum UTC error at the reading times is ≤60 seconds, identifies its authority and validity interval, and covers every reading in the proposed ordering. A labelled simulated fixture can carry an explicit, separately identified simulated attestation; `source_mode=SIMULATED` alone never proves a real clock. Persist the attestation ID/version in each analytic input basis. A real gateway lacking this proof retains `SEQUENCE_ORDER_INVERTED` and an unresolved series limit.
2. Treat each reading as the source-time uncertainty interval `[source_time−error, source_time+error]` (or the reported measurement interval widened by error). Strictly disjoint intervals may be ordered independent of gateway admission sequence. Equal or overlapping intervals in one channel remain `REPORTS_OVERLAP`/ambiguous; do not select a winner or silently interpolate. A reading outside attestation validity, across an unproved clock step, or spanning two conflicting clock attestations remains limited. A 60-second cap is an admission ceiling, not an assertion that the current gateway meets it.
3. Never merge sequence epochs by source time alone. The existing multi-epoch default ambiguity and explicit-selection limits remain; a new continuity proof would need separate review. Sequence still authenticates identity/duplication, not source-time order. Keep every refused span and the involved evidence/attestation IDs in the input digest and assessment limitations. Use a new S3 `MODEL_VERSION` and a new S4 `MATCHER_VERSION`; preserve old runs, selections, assessment and Finding revisions.

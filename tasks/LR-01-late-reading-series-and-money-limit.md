## Architect-ruled contract — S3 cohort v4

The reviewed contract is deterministic and fail closed:

1. For each selected `(gateway, source, sequence_epoch, role, asset)` channel, use **source-time order** only when a durable, versioned, tenant/Site/source/epoch-scoped clock attestation (new) proves the source clock's maximum UTC error at the reading times is ≤60 seconds, identifies its authority and validity interval, and covers every reading in the proposed ordering. A labelled simulated fixture can carry an explicit, separately identified simulated attestation; simulated proof cannot validate a real source and `source_mode=SIMULATED` alone proves no clock. Persist the attestation ID/version in each analytic input basis. A real gateway lacking this proof retains `SEQUENCE_ORDER_INVERTED` and an unresolved series limit.
2. For an attested channel, order by each reading's `placed_at` instant (measurement interval end, otherwise source time) ± the attested UTC error, **not** by the whole measurement interval widened by error. Back-to-back measurement intervals that only touch do not overlap. Genuine measurement-interval overlap or overlapping placement-uncertainty intervals in one channel remain `REPORTS_OVERLAP`/ambiguous, even if gateway sequence agrees; do not choose a winner or interpolate. With disjoint placement-uncertainty intervals, source-time order wins even if admission sequence disagrees: a late arrival is not `SEQUENCE_ORDER_INVERTED`. Dense in-order five-minute means must remain usable. A reading outside attestation validity, across an unproved clock step, or spanning conflicting attestations remains limited. The 60-second cap is an admission ceiling, not a claim about this gateway.
3. Never merge sequence epochs by source time alone. The existing multi-epoch default ambiguity and explicit cohort-selection path remain; automatic continuity across epochs is outside LR-01 and needs a separately reviewed public proof. Sequence still authenticates identity/duplication, not source-time order. Keep every refused span and the involved evidence/attestation IDs in the input digest and assessment limitations. Use a new S3 `MODEL_VERSION` and a new S4 `MATCHER_VERSION`; preserve old runs, selections, assessment and Finding revisions.

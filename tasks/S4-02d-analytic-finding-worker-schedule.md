## Schedule, state and S5 contract

1. **Owned Site inventory and cadence.** Configure one deployment-owned timer (Windows Task Scheduler or systemd) at a documented cadence, initially one minute. A server-side orchestrator enumerates active imported tenant/Sites from the authoritative persisted Site registry on every tick, including newly activated Sites; use stable tenant/Site IDs, never a hard-coded demo list or gateway-supplied scope. Define and test the exact active predicate and exclusion of inactive/foreign Sites. Bound Sites per tick, S3 jobs per tick and consequence items per Site, with a durable cursor or fair ordering so a busy Site cannot starve another. The existing S3 worker's global reconcile must still run; expose a bounded drain limit rather than relying on the CLI's default 1000. S4 matching runs only after S3 work relevant to that Site has reached a completed basis; if S3 remains due, leased, failed or deferred, record it as pending and defer that Site's S4/currentness promotion. Match the Site even when no new S3 job exists if a selected assessment/configuration/model basis became dirty. Then drain that Site's consequence work after Finding commits. One tick may leave work pending; later ticks resume it.
2. **Concurrency and recovery.** Use a database claim for the schedule/tick and per-Site processing, or prove an equivalent cross-host exclusion; a local timer's “do not start another instance” is insufficient. Preserve S3's lease/retry and S4-02c's Site claim/append idempotency. A crash releases or expires the claim, and the next tick resumes without duplicate assessment, FindingRevision or consequence basis. Bound wall time per tick and per Site, including a hung `match-findings`/database call; report timeout as failure and leave work retryable. Use a documented retry/backoff for schedule-level failures, including failed Site discovery, S3 worker exceptions and matcher exceptions; do not silently advance success. Do not add a second independent retry mechanism to S3 or financial work rows.
3. **Observable durable state.** Persist or derive a tenant/Site-scoped schedule record with last tick start, last success, last failure (stage, typed code and time), running/claim age, retry due, selected/processed/deferred counts and remaining S3/S4/consequence backlog. Define S4 backlog by a durable dirty basis/watermark or equivalent exact comparison between selected assessment revisions and matched Finding basis, not by counting only queued S3 jobs. Expose a read-only operator report/CLI with Site identity and an aggregate; distinguish `RUNNING`, `BACKLOG`, `RETRY_DUE`, `FAILED`, `STOPPED` (missed heartbeat beyond a declared threshold), and healthy idle. A successful zero-work tick advances the heartbeat. Failed and repeatedly skipped/hung ticks cannot look healthy. Carry S4-02c review I1/I2: show permanently failing consequence attempt count/age and growing backlog without capping or deleting attempts; make repeated Site-claim skips and stale in-flight claims an alarm with a concrete recovery procedure. Document the timer identity, credentials, cadence, timeouts, alert threshold, restart and commands in `backend/README-db.md`.
4. **S5-01 consumption.** Provide a scoped status/watermark read that S5-01 can join to its exact selected half-open Site window: accepted-source watermark, selected S3 run/revision, S4 matched revision/basis and outstanding/failed consequence work plus schedule heartbeat. A first build with outstanding work is `PENDING`; an older exact result with an advanced source/analytic basis or dirty work is `STALE_PROJECTION`; a failed worker with no usable prior result is `FAILED`, with the exact upstream stage/reason available. A stopped or unobserved schedule cannot certify CURRENT, even when a queue query is empty. Only a healthy observed pass after the accepted-source watermark, completed relevant S3 work, matched S4 basis and completed relevant consequence work may permit CURRENT; S5-01 still applies its separate F1 money rule and `CURRENT/PRICED` same-basis check. Keep old IDs and lineage readable. This packet exposes the contract and tests its gate; S5-01 implements the Overview read/UI.

## Risks and carries

- S3's existing global `reconcile_all()` can be expensive as Sites grow; measure a multi-Site tick and keep bounded scheduling truthful. Do not omit reconcile to meet a timer budget.
- S4 matching currently scans a Site's selected reads. Large Sites may require a reviewed bounded cursor or dirty-window selection to meet the tick limit; do not mark a partially matched Site caught up.
- S4-02c retries have no terminal cap: a deterministic failed row can append about 90 attempts/day. Keep the growing count and backlog observable and alertable; do not hide it by reporting only due work.
- A hung consequence runner can hold a Site claim while later ticks report skipped. Treat repeated skips/claim age as a stopped-progress alarm, with operator recovery; do not count skipped as success.
- Backdated configuration/model changes can dirty assessed windows without a fresh evidence job. The S3 reconcile and S4 basis comparison must detect them before CURRENT is possible.

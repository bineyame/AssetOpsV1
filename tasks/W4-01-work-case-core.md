## Exit criteria

- Persist distinct, tenant/Site-scoped Incident, MaintenanceWorkRequest, MaintenanceRecord, MaintenancePlan, WorkEvent and OperationalRecord identities with exact optional Asset and Finding/Action links. Authored chronology, actor/attestation, event order, correction/revision and refusal behavior survive reload. A completed activity remains unverified until separate accepted outcome evidence supports it.
- Publish scoped case create/transition/correction/detail/list APIs and read shapes under an Architect-approved lifecycle and transition table. Unauthorized, cross-Site, wrong-Asset, invalid transition, duplicate event and missing/stale evidence paths refuse or report missing explicitly; no private Lab truth or static mock row becomes product history.
- Show one persisted and reloadable case through a Site list, plus an honest empty or missing-state path on **each** Site list. Preserve exact source, actor, Site/Asset and revision lineage and selected-window/currentness semantics. Do not infer an Incident from a single data gap.
- Freeze identity versus Action, lifecycle, attestation, correction, links, event order, API/read shapes and refusal codes at independent review ACCEPT and merge. W3/W5/W6 consume that contract; changes require a versioned Architect amendment and compatibility proof.

AssetOps · app-wide menu and page organization · proposal for the owner's input · Architect · 20 Nov 2026 · nothing is built yet

Keep the six groups and the loop they follow. Give every object one home, one shape and one way in.

Today's menu was designed with care, and the proposal keeps its groups and order. The contradictions come from the pages under it. Each object is drawn in two or three places, the new executive objects have no home, and each screen invents its own layout. Patching a screen cannot fix that, so the proposal fixes the structure.

1What today's structure is for, and what carries over

Read from the live product (frontend/src/shell/OperatorShellLayout.tsx, ui/NavRail.tsx, shell/operatorSiteTabs.tsx, estate/portfolioWords.ts) and the mock-up's rail.

What it gets right

  • The groups follow the operating loop: what we run (Operations), what we learned (Intelligence), what we do (Work), how we know (Assurance), what it is worth (Business), how it is set up (Admin).
  • Truthful destinations. A menu item appears only when a real page stands behind it (rail rule W1-UX-07).
  • The Site is the unit. Each Site has its own address, is reached from a Sites row, and shows the same tabs whatever its type.
  • Objects with identity get pages with addresses: Finding, Action, Asset, work request.
  • The Lab sits apart in its own shell, so simulator truth never mixes with product evidence.

What carries over unchanged

  • The six group labels and their order (owner, U8).
  • Portfolio first, Sites second: the money question leads.
  • One set of Site tabs for every vertical.
  • Pages with addresses for objects people work on.
  • The scope switch and the period in the top bar. Scope is never a role switch.
  • The Lab apart from the operator's menu.
  • "Not available yet" where data has no source.

Why patching cannot fix it

A
Two or three homes per object. The same Findings, Actions and assets are drawn by a global list, a Site tab and a Portfolio tab, each from different code. So names, statuses, counts and even operators differ (audit C1, C2, C3).
B
The new executive objects have no home. Decisions hang under Portfolio, the plan under "Act first", and verdicts under "What changed" (C9).
C
Shapes are per screen, not per object. Each screen sets its own levels, so pages grow with every Site and the unbuilt ones show stubs (C4, C5, C8).

2The proposed menu

The six groups stay. Three items are added, two merge, one is renamed, and the Lab moves out. Each item answers one person's question and serves one or more of the four objectives: G Grow revenue, O Optimize cost and efficiency, P Protect asset and capital, A Assure performance and returns. These questions guide the design and are never written on a screen.

3Page organization across the app

Every object, whether it is a page or a drawer, its tabs, its home in the menu, and how it links to the others. Each object has one home list; any other surface shows its top three and a "View all" link that keeps the scope.
Hub page (tabs, queue) Object page (its own address) Home list (one per object) Drawer over the page (its own address, Esc closes) Coloured top edge: the menu group that owns it
Page or drawer: one ruleA page when people work through it over days, with a state, an owner and a history (Site, Decision, Plan, Finding, Action, Asset). A drawer when it is read beside its parent and acted on in one step (Incident, Maintenance, Right, Record, a figure's evidence, the inbox).
One drill-down grammarA row opens its object. A figure opens its evidence drawer. "View all" opens the home list, keeping the scope. Each row has one target and never holds other controls.
WayfindingBreadcrumb = home list › Site › object. The menu highlights the owning item: a Decision lights Decisions, not Portfolio. Every tab, filter and drawer has an address, so Back and shared links restore the view.
One store, many scopesAn object is read from one store and drawn by one component. A Site tab or a Portfolio queue is that component scoped. Two screens cannot disagree, because there is only one.
One way to actChoose, Approve, Send back, Accept and Grant: a dialog that says what happens and who is recorded, then a toast with Undo, then the state changes on the page and in History. States are shown as labels, never as buttons.
Nothing unbuilt is reachableA tab, column or tile without content is not drawn. A missing figure shows one "Not available yet" in its place, never a stub page.

7Questions for you, each with my default

Defaults apply if you agree with the direction. Nothing is built in the mock-up until you have given your input.
1 · Decisions and Plan as menu items?
The design's frame rule says "no new menu items" (§2.8). Layer 4's two executive objects then have no home and hang under Portfolio tabs.
Default: yes. Decisions under Intelligence, Plan at the head of Work.
2 · Portfolio from six tabs to three?
Act first repeats the Overview's queue, What changed is not a list of changes, and Scope repeats Evidence. Each moves to its object's home list.
Default: yes. The tabs become Overview, Outcomes, and Demand & capacity.
3 · Site from eight tabs to six, the same for every vertical?
Findings and Work become the Overview queue and the Operations tab. Health and Performance merge.
Default: yes. Overview, Operations, Performance, Financials, Evidence, Setup.
4 · Incidents and Maintenance as two items, not one "Operations" entry?
Pushback on the design's own §2.10. You chose option B, and the two answer different questions. They share one store, so they cannot contradict each other.
Default: two items in Work, plus the Site's Operations tab.
5 · A "Results" item under Assurance?
The executive's reason to come back, and the proof that monitoring tools cannot give. Today the verdicts are scattered across What changed and the Action pages.
Default: yes. Verdicts and the plan's review, by period.
6 · Merge Evidence and Operational records?
Records are the sources that fill the figures; coverage is how complete those sources are. They are one subject, with imports waiting for acceptance beside them.
Default: yes, as "Evidence & records".
7 · Site groups in the scope switch?
At 300 Sites one list is too long, even capped. A group (region, vertical or contract) scopes every list and the Portfolio hub.
Default: yes. Groups are set in Settings; the demo groups by vertical.
8 · Simulator Lab out of the operator's menu?
The live product already keeps it in its own shell; the mock-up drifted. Engineering words one click from the executive cost trust.
Default: yes, in a workspace footer in demo builds only.
9 · Build by shape, not by screen?
Each unit builds one shape and renders every object of its type. Its gate is that every route of that type renders from the shape, with no stub reachable.
Default order: List, Hub, Record page and Drawer, Decision, Plan, Asset, Statement.

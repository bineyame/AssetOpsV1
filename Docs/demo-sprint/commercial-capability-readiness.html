1 AssetOps commercial mission and value thesis

Mission (owner, settled). AssetOps helps infrastructure operators systematically improve commercial and operational performance through integrated data, explainable intelligence, actionable decisions, historical accountability and independently verifiable evidence.

Governing principle (owner). Commercial outcomes define the objectives. Physical and economic drivers explain the opportunities. Coordinated capabilities deliver the interventions. Evidence and verification establish what was achieved. Capability gaps define the work necessary to realise the target system.

The value thesis

Mini-grids, electric bus depots and cold rooms lose money in ways that are physical, recurring and hard to see from a bill or a dashboard. A generator runs when the battery could have carried the load. Solar output is spilled while a paying customer waits for power. A bus leaves the depot short of charge because another vehicle took the shared supply. Stock sits in a warm room because a backup failed to start. Each loss has a physical cause, an operational choice that could change it and a commercial consequence that someone carries.

For the operators AssetOps serves first, these losses sit on thin margins: diesel bought and trucked to remote sites, revenue that depends on reliable supply and on collection, scarce capital for replacing batteries, chargers and compressors, and funders and lenders who pay or lend against verified results. AssetOps does not earn or save money itself. The operator's intervention does. AssetOps creates value by making those interventions found sooner, chosen better and proven afterwards:

Discover

Show each category's state, and find the drivers holding it back, from evidence the operator can trace to a source reading, with honest unknowns where evidence is missing.

Steer

Compare levers on those drivers under the site's physical, contractual and resource constraints, with the categories a lever must not harm made explicit. People choose and act.

Assure

Establish independently what changed, technically first and commercially second, and read it back against the same category.

The commercial value of AssetOps to a customer is the value of the better interventions it causes, plus the cost of poor interventions it prevents, plus the value of credible evidence (cheaper finance, results-based payments, contract standing). Every claim in the product says which of these it is and how far it has been proven.

2.2 Risk, opportunity and cost cut across the categories

The owner's rule is that risk, opportunity and cost are cross-cutting concerns. In the product they are labels on items, not places items live:

  • A risk is a driver pointing the wrong way for some category: an interrupted charging session is a risk in Protect asset and capital and, through lost sessions, a risk to Grow revenue.
  • An opportunity is a driver with room to improve some category: idle chargers are an opportunity in Grow revenue.
  • A cost is a money consequence of a driver in whatever category it sits: repeat repair visits are a cost in Optimize cost and efficiency.

The existing Findings lens chips (estate/index.py:24-26, flags COST, RISK and OPPORTUNITY) stay as the filter chips the owner kept. The categories are added beside them, at the data level, through the outcome map (4.2). A filter chip and a category answer different questions: "what kind of signal is this?" and "which business objective does it move?"

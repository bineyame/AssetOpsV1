## Financial attribution and demo labels

Use one claim ladder in Site, executive summary and drilldown, with currency, time window, beneficiary, source IDs, price/version, counterfactual assumptions and exclusions visible together:

| Claim | Demo label and permitted calculation | Promotion evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Modelled opportunity | **Simulated modelled opportunity**: eligible energy, avoided exposure or capacity × declared tariff/cost/risk assumption; gross and bounded where demand, collection or physical loss is unknown. | Accepted simulated observations and explicit scenario/price basis. This is potential, not earnings. |
| Expected intervention value | **Simulated expected value (assumption-based)**: modelled benefit less stated intervention and operating costs, with range and probability only if those inputs are justified. Otherwise show components and “value unavailable.” | Named action, feasible constraint, cost inputs, baseline/counterfactual and uncertainty; no automatic promotion from a recommendation. |
| Technically verified improvement | **Simulated technical improvement verified** only when a frozen domain criterion passes on independent accepted later observations with coverage, comparability, source coherence and guardrails; fail/inconclusive remain visible. | Evaluator result, exact baseline/later evidence and plan versions. This verifies a technical condition, not its monetary cause. |
| Realised value | **Verified realised value: unavailable in simulation.** A paired simulation may show a *modelled difference*, never realized cash or stock value. | In a pilot: accepted field before/after measurements plus relevant sales, invoices, fuel receipts, transactions, inventory/quality or service records, matched boundaries and approved confounder treatment. Record beneficiary sign-off and uncertainty. |

For an honest simulated before/after comparison, freeze the same initial physical stocks, exogenous weather/load/route/door schedule, observation mapping, prices and source-quality profile; change only the declared intervention. Generate both worlds independently, project through device observations, gateway and accepted evidence, and let AssetOps compute the comparison without private-state access. Retain both run/input hashes and mismatched or missing evidence as inconclusive. This demonstrates the causal mechanics of the simulator and AssetOps' ability to infer from observations; it does not establish field causal effect or realized money.

## The important workflow distinction

Mini-grid has the strongest complete *demonstration* chain: registered simulation → public observations → gateway queue/HTTPS ingestion → accepted evidence → dispatch/Finding → bounded fuel or productive-load consequence → Action trial → later accepted-window technical evaluation. `actions/verification.py:evaluate` computes `VERIFIED`, `INEFFECTIVE` or `INCONCLUSIVE` from a frozen one-hour baseline/later plan and source-coherent evidence; it can append or withdraw `RESOLVED`. `actions/lifecycle.py` says this is not a causal or savings claim. `finding_episode.py` can mark a generator-support Finding `NO_LONGER_SUPPORTED` and reopen on recurrence; that is distinct from Action completion and from verified value. Cold and EV have more domain assessment and Work UI than the earlier assessment said, but neither has a domain-wide independent financial verifier. No inspected path verifies realized collections, cash savings, spoiled stock, missed bus revenue or investment return.

The highest leverage small changes are (1) a source mapping/quality contract with original payload and version across one external feed, (2) a cross-vertical claim ladder in the UI and reports that never promotes scenario/estimated/technical to realized money, (3) one customer-specific business record join for a selected outcome, and (4) repeatable linked baseline/intervention/later evidence. Do not introduce a generic analytics framework for these. See [integration baseline](integration-pilot-baseline.md) and [simulation assessment](simulation-evolution-assessment.md).

## Supportable promises and blockers

**Supportable today, qualified:** an integrated simulated source-to-evidence chain, exact source and revision drilldown, selected-window site/portfolio views, bounded mini opportunity and fuel models, EV session energy/tariff estimate, cold room-air exposure with unpriced state, and a narrow independently evaluated mini technical trial. Focused pure/host tests passed for parts of this chain; Postgres reads and a joined customer-facing rehearsal remain unverified here. **Aspirational:** real OEM/protocol compatibility, fleet bus fulfillment, settled commercial gain, meter-to-cash, product spoilage, SoH, full ROI, compliance certification. Field calibration, customer rights, topology, price books, actor security, and accepted real baseline remain pilot gates. The separate final recapture and in-review branch states are not equivalent to merged acceptance.

## Decision summary (§11)

1. **Differentiation and demo leads:** the mini-grid simulated generator-support chain reaches a narrow independent technical verdict with provenance and modeled fuel consequence, subject to a joined rehearsal. Cold and EV have credible assessment/Work components; their full lifecycle lead outcomes require domain verification rules and later evidence. The ready-now and short-effort groups above determine demo leads; the rest are roadmap.
2. **Partial or unsupported outcomes:** collected productive sales, site contribution/ROI, bus fulfillment and cost per revenue km, cold inventory value/service margin, asset capital protection and verified realised value need independent commercial records and often field calibration. No simulated amount is realised money.
3. **Highest leverage:** a cross-vertical claim ladder; cold/EV frozen technical criteria and recurrence; linked long-history before/after evidence; one versioned external-source adapter with negative controls and exact lineage; a selected customer business-record join.
4. **Integration order and coverage:** the only demonstrated supported profile is registered canonical gateway/HTTPS. Provide each subsequently proven profile as its own site in one demo portfolio. Start with a buyer-authorized EMS/OEM or CSMS read-only API/export and one documented Modbus TCP or independent sensor route where source coverage requires it. All map through the existing gateway, ingress and provenance; CAN/MQTT/file are candidate emulations with separate proof gates.
5. **Local protocol design:** a read-only host-lab or edge collector decodes authorized frames or polls pinned registers, preserves raw payload/mapping version and publishes canonical reports through the gateway. Wire emulation proves its documented test profile, not field model/firmware compatibility.
6. **Simulator and verification:** extend mini forcing beyond 475 minutes; prove state-continuous linked windows and episode/off/gap behavior across weeks/months; measure end-to-end ingest and reads. Raise the 100,000-minute parser cap only if single runs are needed and measured. Add cold/EV independent evaluators; preserve technical and financial verdict separation. Checkpoint only if measured restart cost warrants it.
7. **Reusable/deferred:** reuse source mapping, replay quality, frozen criteria, recurrence and claim labels. Defer full OCPP server, broad OEM/CAN drivers, generic digital twin, automated dispatch, speculative SoH/spoilage and a full pilot roadmap.
8. **Demo versus pilot:** a simulated demo establishes mechanics and bounded opportunity; field value requires authorized feeds, calibrated boundaries, security, business baselines and customer validation.

**Owner decisions remaining for this assessment:** none. The owner has authorized the lead-outcome rule, independent checks in each vertical and portfolio profile coverage. Later planning must choose the first buyer/source and approve any measured run-limit or retention contract change when a concrete proposal exists.

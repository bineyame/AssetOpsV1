## The important workflow distinction

Mini-grid has the strongest complete *demonstration* chain: registered simulation → public observations → gateway queue/HTTPS ingestion → accepted evidence → dispatch/Finding → bounded fuel or productive-load consequence → Action trial → later accepted-window technical evaluation. `actions/verification.py:evaluate` computes `VERIFIED`, `INEFFECTIVE` or `INCONCLUSIVE` from a frozen one-hour baseline/later plan and source-coherent evidence; it can append or withdraw `RESOLVED`. `actions/lifecycle.py` says this is not a causal or savings claim. `finding_episode.py` can mark a generator-support Finding `NO_LONGER_SUPPORTED` and reopen on recurrence; that is distinct from Action completion and from verified value. Cold and EV have more domain assessment and Work UI than the earlier assessment said, but neither has a domain-wide independent financial verifier. No inspected path verifies realized collections, cash savings, spoiled stock, missed bus revenue or investment return.

The highest leverage small changes are (1) a source mapping/quality contract with original payload and version across one external feed, (2) a cross-vertical claim ladder in the UI and reports that never promotes scenario/estimated/technical to realized money, (3) one customer-specific business record join for a selected outcome, and (4) repeatable linked baseline/intervention/later evidence. Do not introduce a generic analytics framework for these. See [integration baseline](integration-pilot-baseline.md) and [simulation assessment](simulation-evolution-assessment.md).

## Supportable promises and blockers

**Supportable today, qualified:** an integrated simulated source-to-evidence chain, exact source and revision drilldown, selected-window site/portfolio views, bounded mini opportunity and fuel models, EV session energy/tariff estimate, cold room-air exposure with unpriced state, and a narrow independently evaluated mini technical trial. Focused pure/host tests passed for parts of this chain; Postgres reads and a joined customer-facing rehearsal remain unverified here. **Aspirational:** real OEM/protocol compatibility, fleet bus fulfillment, settled commercial gain, meter-to-cash, product spoilage, SoH, full ROI, compliance certification. Field calibration, customer rights, topology, price books, actor security, and accepted real baseline remain pilot gates. The separate final recapture and in-review branch states are not equivalent to merged acceptance.

For future planning, carry the stable IDs through the complete trace, rather than converting every gap above into sprint work:

`Demo` here means credible simulated operation; `pilot preparation` is access/mapping/KPI agreement; `pilot execution` requires accepted real baseline/intervention/later evidence and customer validation; `post-pilot` is reusable onboarding/support. None of the demo criteria alone establishes field commercial value.

## Decision summary (§11)

1. **Differentiation already present:** public-evidence lineage, honest unknowns, bounded domain analysis, persisted Findings/Work and an independent mini technical verifier form a credible *simulated* operational-to-decision chain.
2. **Partial or unsupported commercial outcomes:** productive sales/collections, site contribution/ROI, bus service and cost per revenue km, cold inventory value/service margin, asset capital protection and verified realized value.
3. **Highest leverage gaps:** one external-source mapping with negative controls, explicit claim ladder, one commercial record join per chosen buyer outcome, and repeatable comparable post-action evidence.
4. **First patterns:** authorized read-only EMS/OEM/CSMS HTTPS export through the gateway, plus an independent sensor or documented local register collector when cloud coverage is inadequate. They reuse ingress and provenance without claiming universal protocol support.
5. **CAN/local route:** decode authorized frames or poll documented registers in a read-only host-lab/edge adapter, map to the existing canonical publication envelope and gateway; field model/firmware compatibility requires separate proof.
6. **Simulator:** lift the 100,000-minute cap only with a measured resource bound; allow scenario repetition and state continuity; distinguish physical steps, observation and delivery clocks; add durable checkpoint only if a benchmark shows restart cost demands it.
7. **Reusable improvements:** versioned source mapping, quality/replay controls, claim labeling, episode recurrence, comparable outcome criteria and source-backed portfolio summaries.
8. **Defer:** full OCPP server, broad CAN/OEM drivers, generalized digital twin/orchestration, automated dispatch, speculative SoH/spoilage/verified saving, and a full pilot roadmap.
9. **Owner decisions:** select the first commercial claim and its acceptable evidence; approve the first external acquisition pattern and funding/permissions for its source; approve any run-limit/retention contract change after measurement; decide whether a mini technical trial is enough for the first demo. Existing contracts remain unchanged here.
10. **Tradeoff:** a simulated demo can show causal mechanics and bounded opportunity quickly; a first pilot needs authorized feeds, calibrated boundaries, security and business baselines before the same labels can refer to field outcomes.

**Owner review requested:** confirm the first buyer outcome, source pattern, and claim boundary before a separate planning phase selects demo scope.

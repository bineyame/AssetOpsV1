### Economic materiality of the leading decisions

These are **illustrative sensitivities, not customer savings or a market forecast**. For each row, compare the same beneficiary, currency and window; if the input range is unavailable, leave the amount symbolic. A decision can be important yet immaterial at a particular site.

| Buyer decision / beneficiary | Unit economics and illustrative sensitivity | When unattractive; stronger attribution |
|---|---|---|
| Mini productive demand / operator | Potential contribution = realizable incremental kWh × (collectible tariff − marginal generation, delivery and commercial cost) − enabling cost. For an *assumed* 100–500 eligible kWh/month and net 0.05–0.15 currency units/kWh, the contribution before enabling cost spans 5–75 units/month; this is a sensitivity, not observed demand. | Zero if no paying demand, reserve breach, high marginal diesel cost or collection failure. Require customer schedule, metered delivery, tariff/version, marginal cost and settlements to strengthen the claim. |
| Mini dispatch and supply cost / operator | Avoidable cost = comparable served energy × change in fuel/supply cost per kWh, less intervention cost. An assumed 1–5% reduction in a declared 1,000-unit/month eligible fuel bill is 10–50 units/month *before* reserve and maintenance effects. | Immaterial at low generator duty; unattractive if reserve, reliability or wear worsens. Require measured litres or purchases, served-energy boundary, comparable load/weather and accepted later evidence. Runtime alone is not fuel saving. |
| EV bus charge decision / fleet operator | Required energy = usable battery capacity × (target SoC − initial SoC), adjusted for losses; feasible delivery is bounded by charger, shared depot supply and time to departure. An *assumed* identified 200 kWh bus battery at 20% SoC targeting 60% needs at least 80 kWh; over a 2-hour window that means ≥40 kW net average, so a 30 kW depot/charger cap leaves ≥20 kWh unmet even before losses. Tariff-shift opportunity = eligible kWh × tariff difference less scheduling cost. | Little value if the vehicle has slack, supply is unconstrained or the tariff spread is negligible. Vehicle/battery ID, SoC, roster, charger session, depot meter and tariffs support modeled readiness; trip fulfillment and cost per revenue km need service and odometer records. |
| Cold lot exposure / goods owner | Potential exposure = declared lot value × qualified at-risk fraction under an approved product/time/temperature rule. A hypothetical 10,000-unit lot at 0–20% risk gives 0–2,000 units *exposed*, not spoiled or saved. | No material exposure for empty/low-value rooms or harmless excursions. Need calibrated product/room probes, lot location, quality adjudication and sales/rejection evidence for stronger attribution. |
| Cold paid capacity / storage provider | Potential service contribution = additional billable pallet-days × contract rate − incremental cooling, handling and acquisition cost. Ten *assumed* billable pallet-days at 20 units/day with 80 units incremental cost yield 120 units before fixed costs. | Unattractive without paying demand or if cooling/handling exceeds receipts. Require occupancy, capacity, contracts, invoices, independent kWh and cost allocation; physical occupancy is not paid utilization. |

The mini-grid frame is **site and portfolio improvement potential**, with productive use and supply-cost reduction both visible. EV starts with bus fleet economics, while swap and charging-service economics remain separate segments. Cold starts with the goods owner; provider occupancy and cost per billable unit remain a distinct opportunity. No actual pilot device or business source is approved: plausible first routes are a read-only mini EMS/OEM API or export (local metering alternative), EV CSMS export plus fleet records, and a cold independent logger/controller export (approved gateway alternative). Compatibility and schedule belong to customer discovery and the separate integration assessment.

## Financial attribution and demo labels

Use one claim ladder in Site, executive summary and drilldown, with currency, time window, beneficiary, source IDs, price/version, counterfactual assumptions and exclusions visible together:

| Claim | Demo label and permitted calculation | Promotion evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Simulated modeled opportunity | Eligible energy, avoided exposure or capacity × declared tariff/cost/risk assumption; gross and bounded where demand, collection or physical loss is unknown. | Accepted simulated observations and explicit scenario/price basis. This is potential, not earnings. |
| Assumption-based expected intervention value | Modeled benefit less stated intervention and operating costs, with range and probability only if those inputs are justified. Otherwise show components and “value unavailable.” | Named action, feasible constraint, cost inputs, baseline/counterfactual and uncertainty; no automatic promotion from a recommendation. |
| Independently verified simulated technical improvement | Only when a frozen domain criterion passes on independent accepted later observations with coverage, comparability, source coherence and guardrails; fail/inconclusive remain visible. | Evaluator result, exact baseline/later evidence and plan versions. This verifies a technical condition, not its monetary cause. |
| Verified realized field commercial value | **Unavailable in simulation.** A paired simulation may show a *modeled difference*, never realized cash or stock value. | In a pilot: accepted field before/after measurements plus relevant sales, invoices, fuel receipts, transactions, inventory/quality or service records, matched boundaries and approved confounder treatment. Record beneficiary sign-off and uncertainty. |

For an honest simulated before/after comparison, freeze the same initial physical stocks, exogenous weather/load/route/door schedule, observation mapping, prices and source-quality profile; change only the declared intervention. Generate both worlds independently, project through device observations, gateway and accepted evidence, and let AssetOps compute the comparison without private-state access. Retain both run/input hashes and mismatched or missing evidence as inconclusive. This demonstrates the causal mechanics of the simulator and AssetOps' ability to infer from observations; it does not establish field causal effect or realized money.

## The important workflow distinction

Mini-grid has the strongest complete *demonstration* chain: registered simulation → public observations → gateway queue/HTTPS ingestion → accepted evidence → dispatch/Finding → bounded fuel or productive-load consequence → Action trial → later accepted-window technical evaluation. `actions/verification.py:evaluate` computes `VERIFIED`, `INEFFECTIVE` or `INCONCLUSIVE` from a frozen one-hour baseline/later plan and source-coherent evidence; it can append or withdraw `RESOLVED`. `actions/lifecycle.py` says this is not a causal or savings claim. `finding_episode.py` can mark a generator-support Finding `NO_LONGER_SUPPORTED` and reopen on recurrence; that is distinct from Action completion and from verified value. Cold and EV have more domain assessment and Work UI than the earlier assessment said, but neither has a domain-wide independent financial verifier. No inspected path verifies realized collections, cash savings, spoiled stock, missed bus revenue or investment return.

The highest leverage small changes are (1) a source mapping/quality contract with original payload and version across one external feed, (2) a cross-vertical claim ladder in the UI and reports that never promotes scenario/estimated/technical to realized money, (3) one customer-specific business record join for a selected outcome, and (4) repeatable linked baseline/intervention/later evidence. Do not introduce a generic analytics framework for these. See [integration baseline](integration-pilot-baseline.md) and [simulation assessment](simulation-evolution-assessment.md).

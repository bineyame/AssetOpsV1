## The important workflow distinction

Mini-grid has the strongest complete *demonstration* chain: registered simulation → public observations → gateway queue/HTTPS ingestion → accepted evidence → dispatch/Finding → bounded fuel or productive-load consequence → Action trial → later accepted-window technical evaluation. `actions/verification.py:evaluate` computes `VERIFIED`, `INEFFECTIVE` or `INCONCLUSIVE` from a frozen one-hour baseline/later plan and source-coherent evidence; it can append or withdraw `RESOLVED`. `actions/lifecycle.py` says this is not a causal or savings claim. `finding_episode.py` can mark a generator-support Finding `NO_LONGER_SUPPORTED` and reopen on recurrence; that is distinct from Action completion and from verified value. Cold and EV have more domain assessment and Work UI than the earlier assessment said, but neither has a domain-wide independent financial verifier. No inspected path verifies realized collections, cash savings, spoiled stock, missed bus revenue or investment return.

The highest leverage small changes are (1) a source mapping/quality contract with original payload and version across one external feed, (2) a cross-vertical claim ladder in the UI and reports that never promotes scenario/estimated/technical to realized money, (3) one customer-specific business record join for a selected outcome, and (4) repeatable linked baseline/intervention/later evidence. Do not introduce a generic analytics framework for these. See [integration baseline](integration-pilot-baseline.md) and [simulation assessment](simulation-evolution-assessment.md).

## Supportable promises and blockers

**Supportable today, qualified:** an integrated simulated source-to-evidence chain, exact source and revision drilldown, selected-window site/portfolio views, bounded mini opportunity and fuel models, EV session energy/tariff estimate, cold room-air exposure with unpriced state, and a narrow independently evaluated mini technical trial. Focused pure/host tests passed for parts of this chain; Postgres reads and a joined customer-facing rehearsal remain unverified here. **Aspirational:** real OEM/protocol compatibility, fleet bus fulfillment, settled commercial gain, meter-to-cash, product spoilage, SoH, full ROI, compliance certification. Field calibration, customer rights, topology, price books, actor security, and accepted real baseline remain pilot gates. The separate final recapture and in-review branch states are not equivalent to merged acceptance.

## Decision summary (§11)

1. **Commercial lead:** frame mini-grid around potential site/portfolio margin: qualified productive-use contribution, generation cost, then a proven narrow technical intervention. Generator-support reserve has the strongest simulated independent verdict, subject to a joined DB/browser rehearsal; its fuel amount remains modeled. Portfolio contribution and ROI remain unsupported.
2. **EV choice:** a versioned, explicitly simulated vehicle charging requirement and departure window offers more bus-buyer differentiation per bounded engineering effort than charger verification alone. Existing sessions, tariffs, faults and power sharing supply the technical base. Add an EV later technical verdict only when it answers the selected fleet decision; neither modeled readiness nor charger uptime proves a fulfilled trip.
3. **Cold choice:** for a goods owner, qualify lot exposure and value as an assumption-based scenario; for a storage provider, investigate occupancy and cost per billable unit first. Thermal recovery verification is valuable when tied to an approved service or quality decision. Room-air evidence alone proves neither spoilage nor saved inventory.
4. **Shared proof and deployment:** reuse Findings, Work, source lineage and the claim ladder. Verify fully in mini-grid and extend EV/cold verification selectively. The only demonstrated integration profile is registered canonical gateway/HTTPS; any buyer-authorized API/export, Modbus or independent sensor route needs its own versioned mapping, negative controls and proof before it becomes a supported profile. Simulated paired histories need frozen inputs and accepted later evidence; measured limits and parser/checkpoint changes require a concrete need. Defer broad protocol servers, generic digital twins and speculative asset-loss models.
5. **Claim boundary:** bounded simulated opportunity, expected value, technical improvement and realized field value remain distinct. Collected sales, cost per revenue km, paid cooling yield, inventory loss, capital savings and ROI require independent business records, calibrated boundaries and customer validation. No focused test or simulated amount establishes field value.

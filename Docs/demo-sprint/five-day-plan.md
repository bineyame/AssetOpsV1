# Demo sprint: five-day execution plan

**Proposal for owner review, not authorization to implement.** The deadline is **Friday 30 October 2026 EOD**, not a 30-hour cap. The older Hardware Pack 04 estimate is planning history. Calendar time, review and integration gates determine scope. The three **Tier A** paired stories (M1, C1, E1) are roughly **31–47 engineering hours before contingency** including shared bootstrap/release. At most **two independent implementation slices** may run at once after owner approval; shared changes and the full suite run serially. The scenario minimum is **per vertical**: five mini-grid, three cold-chain and three EV choices, eleven total. It is a selection minimum, not a promise to build eleven source-to-money chains by Friday. **Tier B** (M4, E2, M2, C2) is a conditional short-diagnostic backlog; **Tier C** (M3, M5, C3, E3) is narrated discovery without numbers. A broken evidence chain removes that story from client use.

## Verification and presentation gates

For **each Tier A hero**, use the same chain: frozen scenario and private oracle → device observation transform → sealed gateway outbox → authenticated HTTPS batch and receipt → accepted evidence/source binding → domain assessment and Finding → money or explicit unpriced state → persisted Site/Portfolio read → exact provenance drawer. Hash frozen inputs and accepted outputs; compare reruns. Private oracle is test-only. Test one positive and one negative case: mini missing/contradictory feeder or tariff; EV incomplete/duplicate session and missing meter; cold missing probe/door alternative/open episode. Check energy or heat conservation, state constraints, event order, clock/gap handling, source provenance, exact Decimal arithmetic, no cross-currency/overlap rollup, and counterfactual input equivalence. For later runs, say **“simulated comparison”**; “verified improvement” requires an actual post-intervention accepted field window and guardrails. A Tier B claim must open the accepted source and preserve unknowns; Tier C is an unquantified data request.

The demo follows **business context → discovery → investigation → consequence → intervention → evidence → pilot**. A presenter should use the 12–15-minute sequences in `scenario-options.md`; the screen path is Portfolio → domain Site Overview/Performance → Finding/Session/Excursion → Financials or unpriced consequence → Scenario/Action → Evidence → pilot ask. Keep one visible missing-state fallback for each story. Prioritize the owner's current UI bar: clear hierarchy, readable copy, accessible focus/keyboard, and narrow-width overflow fixes within current routes. For new decisions use the v6.9 mock as layout reference and label any material departure for owner review. Do not put unsupported mock data into the demo.

**Marketing dependency:** a reusable core deck needs one tested product thesis, source-to-claim diagram, three proved decision snapshots, trust/limits and pilot CTA. Each vertical insert needs a topology, buyer decision and exact “what we need from your systems” list. Recordings require a stable run, narration and approved claim language; the website/LinkedIn paragraph and personalized outreach derive from those same claims. Pilot propositions specify buyer, permitted read-only data, 60–90-day success threshold, integration responsibility, and exclusions. If DS-05 is late, deliver only a one-page discovery brief and screen stills of accepted states; do not spend demo time on polish.

## Architect recommendation

1. **Readiness today:** AssetOps is ready for internal simulated technical walkthroughs and selective prospect discovery, but not yet for three repeatable, meeting-ready end-to-end demos. No field integration is established.
2. **Strongest capabilities:** the private-truth/public-evidence separation and authenticated durable ingest; bounded mini-grid, EV and cold assessments; revisioned provenance and honest unknown states; mini-grid scenario arithmetic and cross-domain Portfolio reads.
3. **Ranked heroes and hook:** (1) mini-grid productive-use kWh and local-currency gross tariff ceiling for a solar-hybrid operator, with collection unproved; (2) cold backup failure preceding bounded room-air exposure for a horticulture/dairy store, with stock value only if quality supplies the rule; (3) EV connector availability masking aborted energy delivery for a boda-boda/swap depot, with vehicles and fares uncounted. Each has a specific minute-5-to-7 reveal and 12–15-minute script in `scenario-options.md`.
4. **Additional scenarios and cut line:** Five mini-grid options are M1 productive use, M2 diesel dispatch, M3 prepaid collection, M4 remote portfolio O&M and M5 connection/investor/RBF evidence. Three cold options are C1 backup response, C2 handling heat and C3 regulated stock; three EV options are E1 delivery, E2 weak-feed sharing and E3 depot residual. A = M1/C1/E1 complete paired evidence. B = M4/E2/M2/C2, at most two short diagnostics after Thursday's A gate. C = M3/M5/C3/E3 narrated discovery without fabricated numbers. M2 cannot claim litres or saving; M5 cannot certify eligibility; C3 cannot dispose stock; E3 cannot label theft.
5. **Three most important gaps:** no combined idempotent portfolio bootstrap/reset; no accepted paired baseline/intervention producer for cold and EV; no customer collection, inventory-quality or vehicle/roster join, so monetary and service claims remain bounded.
6. **Minimum intelligence:** accepted observations with source/time/quality; one causal assessment and negative control; explicit selected window; a feasible constrained alternative; a price/stock assumption where justified or an unpriced state; traceable evidence and uncertainty in the Site UI.
7. **Five-day reality:** calendar deadline is Friday 30 October EOD, with at most two independent implementation slices active. Three selected scenarios **per vertical** are the minimum; three Tier A complete stories are the credible Friday build target. Tier B is conditional on existing source/read reuse and rehearsal time. The older 30-hour cap does not govern this iteration. A failing chain narrows the client-ready set rather than licensing a scripted result.
8. **Defer:** all new generic integration/optimization infrastructure, OEM certification, wire-level OCPP/Modbus/MQTT claims, field savings, vehicle-trip loss, product spoilage, battery SoH and broad mock-screen completion until customer data warrants them.
9. **Customer validation needed:** mini-grid meter topology, flexible load, local tariff and prepaid/mobile-money settlement; cold probe placement/calibration, backup logging and quality-approved inventory rule; EV CSMS/battery/depot identity, charge request and departure roster; across all, read rights, cadence, baseline and buyer value threshold.

If all pass by Thursday midday, try M4 and E2 first; promote M2 or C2 only when a higher B item fails its reuse test and its own accepted read is already available.

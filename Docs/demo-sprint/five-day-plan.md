## Verification and presentation gates

For **each** hero, use the same chain: frozen scenario and private oracle → device observation transform → sealed gateway outbox → authenticated HTTPS batch and receipt → accepted evidence/source binding → domain assessment and Finding → money or explicit unpriced state → persisted Site/Portfolio read → exact provenance drawer. Hash the frozen inputs and accepted outputs; compare reruns. Private oracle is test-only. Test one positive and one negative case: mini missing/contradictory feeder or tariff; EV incomplete/duplicate session and missing meter; cold missing probe/door alternative/open episode. Check energy or heat conservation, state constraints, event order, clock/gap handling, source provenance, exact Decimal arithmetic, no cross-currency/overlap rollup, and counterfactual input equivalence. For later runs, say **“simulated comparison”**; “verified improvement” requires an actual post-intervention accepted field window and guardrails.

The demo follows **business context → discovery → investigation → consequence → intervention → evidence → pilot**. A presenter should use the 12–15-minute sequences in `scenario-options.md`; the screen path is Portfolio → domain Site Overview/Performance → Finding/Session/Excursion → Financials or unpriced consequence → Scenario/Action → Evidence → pilot ask. Keep one visible missing-state fallback for each story. Prioritize the owner's current UI bar: clear hierarchy, readable copy, accessible focus/keyboard, and narrow-width overflow fixes within current routes. For new decisions use the v6.9 mock as layout reference and label any material departure for owner review. Do not put unsupported mock data into the demo.

**Marketing dependency:** a reusable core deck needs one tested product thesis, source-to-claim diagram, three proved decision snapshots, trust/limits and pilot CTA. Each vertical insert needs a topology, buyer decision and exact “what we need from your systems” list. Recordings require a stable run, narration and approved claim language; the website/LinkedIn paragraph and personalized outreach derive from those same claims. Pilot propositions specify buyer, permitted read-only data, 60–90-day success threshold, integration responsibility, and exclusions. If DS-05 is late, deliver only a one-page discovery brief and screen stills of accepted states; do not spend demo time on polish.

## Architect recommendation

1. **Readiness today:** AssetOps is ready for internal simulated technical walkthroughs and selective prospect discovery, but not yet for three repeatable, meeting-ready end-to-end demos. No field integration is established.
2. **Strongest capabilities:** the private-truth/public-evidence separation and authenticated durable ingest; bounded mini-grid, EV and cold assessments; revisioned provenance and honest unknown states; mini-grid scenario arithmetic and cross-domain Portfolio reads.
3. **Three heroes:** mini-grid stranded midday PV for a feasible flexible load; EV charger availability masking aborted/short energy delivery; cold backup-response delay leading to bounded room-temperature exposure. Each gives a distinct operational decision with observable evidence.
4. **Rejected or parked options:** diesel-efficiency savings need reliable fuel evidence; battery reserve economics need degradation/service validation; EV power-sharing needs fleet schedule to prove readiness; depot meter residual is a useful drilldown but weaker decision; cold compressor degradation and spoilage claims exceed the modeled/observed basis. Door-driven cold exposure is a fallback, not a second hero.
5. **Three most important gaps:** no combined idempotent story/portfolio bootstrap and reset; no paired, accepted baseline/intervention producer for cold and EV; no qualified cold monetary basis or vehicle departure join, so commercial language must stay bounded.
6. **Minimum intelligence:** accepted observations with source/time/quality; one causal assessment and negative control; explicit selected window; a feasible constrained alternative; a price/stock assumption where justified or an unpriced state; traceable evidence and uncertainty in the Site UI.
7. **Five-day reality:** at 35–48 available hours and stable infrastructure, three honest simulated decision stories plus rehearsal are plausible. Under the proposed 30-hour cap, deliver mini and cold deeply, EV as a limited underdelivery diagnostic, and defer cold valuation, polished media and protocol work.
8. **Defer:** all new generic integration/optimization infrastructure, OEM certification, wire-level OCPP/Modbus/MQTT claims, field savings, vehicle-trip loss, product spoilage, battery SoH and broad mock-screen completion until customer data warrants them.
9. **Customer validation needed:** mini-grid meter topology, flexible load and collection tariff; EV CSMS/vehicle/depot identity, departure schedule and business cost; cold probe placement, backup event logging, inventory exposure rule; across all three, read rights, cadence, baseline and buyer value threshold.
10. **My priority:** prove one complete mini-grid decision first, then cold causal exposure, then EV short delivery; reserve Friday for repeatability and a truthful sales narration. Ask prospects for representative authorized exports before choosing any hardware adapter.

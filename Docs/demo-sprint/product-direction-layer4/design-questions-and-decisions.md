## Architecture decisions taken (owner may overrule)

| # | Conflict | Decision | Justification |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | What a decision is anchored on. Rev 3 anchored decisions on Finding threads | a decision anchors on a **factor key** (site, factor, cause); threads, reconciliation differences, customer evidence and contract events attach as evidence on the factor | a decision changes a factor; many factors (occupancy, customer demand, contract deadlines) have no physical Finding |
| A2 | `chain/period_read.py:138-139` refuses stored totals (D-MONEY.3), but the chain needs totals | the chain read recomputes totals from stored lines on every read; D-MONEY.3 is respected | keeps the existing money rule; monthly lines are cheap to recompute |
| A3 | Outcome map rows today map a signal straight to an outcome | rework the map into the structure registry (outcome → factor relations down to leaves; each signal names the factor it observes; MOVES/CHECKED become the brief's L2.5 roles), same registry form and start-up integrity check | outcomes must be computed from their factors; without the structure between, state and money cannot be computed once |
| A4 | CHECKED is text and counts | obligation bounds on factors (hard / costed / advisory; unknown → refuse / conditional) evaluated on counterfactual chains before recommendation; verification guardrails generated from the same rows; a CHECKED declaration without a policy fails the start-up check | safeguards must act before a recommendation |
| A5 | Backend may not import the simulator, but forward replay needs its kernel | adapter in `host/` behind a **new** port method returning a neutral projection; the existing `run_to_end` → `LabProjection` stays untouched | the dependency direction is a deliberate structural guard; a new method avoids changing a protected seam |
| A6 | Commercial intake vs existing intake (reading upload refuses late intervals and keeps only a hash; the evidence ledger is first-write-wins) | one new commercial record store with raw payload, source-versioned revisions and four clocks; telemetry stays in the evidence ledger | commercial facts arrive late and are corrected by nature |
| A7 | Two vocabularies: the existing source-basis columns named `evidence_class` (0042:50, 0043:37) and the new economic class | the new axis is `money_class` in code; client screens show plain words | different questions must not share a name |
| A8 | The reserve trial (30 → 25) is the only intervention and fails Kobo's dawn adequacy | keep it as one option generator; add per-option controller trials (stop threshold) with their own rule rows and an APPLY path for the `stop-soc-threshold` property (`sites/models.py:167`) | the economically right lever must be executable; the action table is not generalised into an engine |
| A9 | Embakasi's schedule had been valued against October's bill (rev 3 R1) | value every option against **no action under the same prices and contracts** (valuation identity M3); the November tier's 706.80/month belongs to no action | otherwise a tariff change is sold as a product effect |
| A10 | Mto huller connection and the November crew limit (rev 3 R2) | the service drop and meter are fitted by the connection contractor inside the 1,200 connection cost; not crew work | resolves the visit count in all four plan cases |
| A11 | Approval actor | a named human principal approves options and records rights events; SYSTEM_DEMO only for fixture replay, labelled | the brief requires explicit human approval |
| A12 | Private oracle | stays only in the manifest; the mock-up's fixture generator strips it | R1: the oracle is private |
| A13 | Attribution of products (litres × price, kWh × tariff) in the bridge | attribution rule for products: volume effect at plan price, then price effect at actual volume, shown on screen | the brief (S2) requires the identity but leaves the rule open; a declared rule makes the bridge reproducible |
| A14 | The brief asks the mock-up to demonstrate "filters, site selection, role emphasis" (C1 D6); the owner rules out role switches, extra filters and new menus (17 Nov) | no filter controls and no role emphasis control; site selection by navigation; each question answered by a section of the loop | owner direction overrides the brief's example list; the requirement it served (each role finds its answer) is met by the structure |
| A15 | Protect as a structure (rev 4 review H1) | Protect is declared as measured factors with units, period, target, coverage and Σ composition (unserved critical kWh, obligation-hours without redundancy, crate-minutes above limit, departures missed, nights below required, asset-days out of service, liability/penalties as shared money factors, value at stake listed); obligation bounds are derived from these measures | brief L1.1 defines Protect by measured quantities; a set of bound states cannot be composed, planned or drilled |
| A16 | Plan value and unvalued spend (review M2) | the plan shows "modelled change from the valued options" net of their own costs, and must-do/protective spend beside it as not valued in money, with its Protect effect | a headline must not silently omit part of the spend; valuing a must-do in money (e.g. unserved evening energy) would need a value-of-lost-load assumption the brief does not give |
| A17 | Asset value at risk (brief P2: "physical assets at risk … expected loss only with defensible likelihood") | declared as a Protect measure but shown as *not available yet*; deferred to the pilot | the demo world holds no condition data or failure history that would give a defensible likelihood; showing a value without one would breach the brief's own rule |
| A18 | O2 and A4 (re-review LY-1) | O2 declared per vertical as measured factors (availability, field visits, cooling availability, crate-days without excursion, deliverable energy used (import-limited), departures per charger); A4 is the evidence pack, a reading of A1–A3 with lineage, built in the pilot slice; attribution rules renamed AR1–AR5 | Optimize must be built from factors like the other outcomes; A4 is a deliverable over existing measures, not a new quantity |
| A19 | Must-do vs protective (re-review L2) | must-do = no action fails a hard bound (Kisiwa repair, Thika stopgap, Kasarani stopgap), funded first; protective = preserves a right or evidence (Kobo battery evidence), funded next by deadline | the allocator treats them differently, so the class must follow the bound, not the wording |
| A20 | Replay figures on screen (OE-6, N-M2) | every figure resting on a replay, and every value computed from one (plan values, switch cases, sensitivities, downsides), carries "design value: the replay has not yet been run on site data" wherever it appears — card, table, path card or drawer — until the replay spike passes; an assumption (Kirinyaga's recovery share) carries the assumption label instead | the documents disclosed it; the screen must too, because the headline plan value includes these figures |
| A21 | Calibration lane and client exposure (CP-3) | three read-only spikes (Kobo forward replay, Mto headroom replay, Embakasi schedule replay) run first, in parallel with slice 1, each replacing only its own figures; plan values built from them lose the label only when all their spikes pass; client-exposure rule per Q13 | the spikes depend on no slice; the strongest demo claims should not wait behind slices 1–3, and each figure needs its own exit |
| A22 | Per-option Protect effects (CP-2) | declared per decision in the manifest (`protect_effects`) and shown on decision pages; must-do and protective spend names the measure it moves | Mechanic 3 promises that the structure shows each option's effect; Protect is part of the structure since rev 5 |
| A23 | Illustration figures derived, not hand-written (owner, ninth message; R8-M1/M2) | the manifest declares structures, option factor changes and plan inputs; `evaluate_structure.py` evaluates them and writes `derived-values.json`; the mock-up reads computed figures from it; the checker asserts stated figures equal evaluated ones | the demonstration must show the mechanics structurally; hand-written results let date and coverage errors through (Kisiwa restore date, Mto Protect effect) |
